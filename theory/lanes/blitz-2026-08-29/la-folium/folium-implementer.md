# M-INDEX-LA-folium — the circle implementer on one fixed kink folium

**Status proposed: PROVED-candidate, with the scope made explicit below.**

## 0. Exact statement and scope

**ASSUME.**

1. `ϱ_0=ϱ_x^{(g)}` is the fixed, bare A2 kink of
   `theory/corner-a-kinks.md` ⟨1⟩9(b): it is the D1(e′) two-sided state with
   tails `A_α,A_β`, `β=g·α`, and invertible junction insertion
   `M=V_α(g)^{-1}`.
2. The selected circle `h_θ=exp(θξ)` is common-unbroken:
   `h_θ∈H_α∩H_β` for every `θ`, and its on-site action is generated by the
   D26 charge `S^z`.  Hypothesis (S) of D2(e) holds at both tails.
3. D26(INT) holds: `e^{2πiS^z}=c𝟙`.

**PROVE.**  In the GNS triple `(H,π,Ω)` of `ϱ_0`, the selected circle
automorphisms have a strongly continuous implementing unitary group
`U(θ)=e^{iθQ̂}`.  Its Stone generator obeys
`e^{2πiQ̂}=e^{2πiq_0}𝟙` for some real `q_0`; consequently `Q̂` is pure point
and `spec Q̂⊆q_0+ℤ`.

**Scope fence.**  “A2/D1(e′) kink” here means the fixed A2 state just named,
not every arbitrary finite-core modification admitted by the general words
of D1(e′).  The proof also works for a finite-core modification whose padded
blocked tensors span `M_χ(ℂ)`; without that full-span condition the argument
below does not claim folium invariance.  The common-unbroken recital is
necessary: if the circle moves either tail label, A2/Claim A2 puts the rotated
state in a disjoint sector, so no implementer in the fixed GNS representation
can exist.

## 1. The finite-block normality lemma

### ⟨1⟩1. LEMMA FOL-LOC (a rotated junction is a local vector)

**ASSUME.**  D1(c), D1(e′), the fixed A2 junction matrix
`M=V_α(g)^{-1}∈GL(χ)`, and a matrix `M'∈GL(χ)` sufficiently near `M`.

**PROVE.**  There is one fixed padded block `Λ` about the junction and a
local operator `B(M')∈𝔄_Λ`, continuous in `M'`, with `B(M)=𝟙`, such that the
normalised vector `π(B(M'))Ω` has exactly the D1(e′) state obtained by
replacing `M` by `M'`.

**⟨2⟩1.** Choose at least the D1(c) injectivity length `n_0` on each side of
the junction.  If `L_a` and `R_b` are the corresponding left `A_α` and right
`A_β` words, the reference blocked matrices are

`P_{ab}=L_aMR_b`.

Their linear span is all of `M_χ(ℂ)`.

*Justification.*  D1(c) says separately that the `L_a` and the `R_b` span
`M_χ(ℂ)`.  Since `M` is invertible by A2, the bilinear products
`LMR`, with `L,R∈M_χ(ℂ)`, span `M_χ(ℂ)`.  This exact finite-dimensional
calculation is named **FOL-LOC.1**.

**⟨2⟩2.** Let `Γ` send a physical operator `B` on the blocked sites to the
tuple of output blocked matrices

`Γ(B)_s := Σ_t ⟨s|B|t⟩P_t`.

Then `Γ` is onto the direct sum of one copy of `M_χ(ℂ)` for every output
physical string `s`.

*Justification.*  For each fixed `s`, the coefficients `⟨s|B|t⟩` are
independent of all other output rows and `span_t P_t=M_χ(ℂ)` by ⟨2⟩1.
This is the row-by-row linear-algebra calculation **FOL-LOC.2**, using D1(c).

**⟨2⟩3.** Fix once and for all a linear right inverse `R_Γ` of `Γ`.  If
`P(M')` denotes the tuple with entries `L_aM'R_b`, put

`B(M') := 𝟙 + R_Γ(P(M')-P(M))`.

Then `Γ(B(M'))=P(M')`, `B(M)=𝟙`, and `M'↦B(M')` is continuous (indeed
linear-affine).

*Justification.*  Finite-dimensional surjectivity from ⟨2⟩2 admits a linear
right inverse; substitution gives the three assertions.  Named exact
calculation **FOL-LOC.3**; D1(c).

**⟨2⟩4.** Applying `B(M')` to the ket block replaces every reference blocked
matrix `P_t` by the desired matrix `L_aM'R_b`; applying `B(M')^*` to the bra
does the adjoint replacement.  Hence, for every local observable `A`,

`⟨π(B(M'))Ω,π(A)π(B(M'))Ω⟩ / ‖π(B(M'))Ω‖²`

is exactly the normalised D1(e′) contraction with junction `M'`.

*Justification.*  Expand the two local physical matrix elements and use
`Γ(B(M'))=P(M')`; the untouched left/right environments are precisely
`l_α,r_β` in D1(e′).  This is the contraction identity **FOL-LOC.4**, citing
D1(e′).

**⟨2⟩5.** For `M'` near `M`, `B(M')` is near `𝟙`, so
`π(B(M'))Ω≠0`; its normalisation is continuous in `M'`.

*Justification.*  ⟨2⟩3, `B(M)=𝟙`, and `‖Ω‖=1` from the GNS convention D1(a).

**⟨2⟩6. QED.**

### ⟨1⟩2. LEMMA FOL-ORB (the circle orbit stays continuously in the folium)

**ASSUME.**  The hypotheses of §0.  Let the selected circle act on the
quasi-local algebra by the sitewise adjoint automorphisms associated with
`u(h_θ)=e^{iθS^z}`.

**PROVE.**  For `θ` near `0`, `ϱ_0` rotated by `θ` is represented by a
continuous unit vector `ξ_θ∈H`, `ξ_0=Ω`.

**⟨2⟩1.**  Apply D2(b) on the two tails.  All virtual gauges telescope away
except at the junction; phases disappear from the state.  Thus rotation by
`h_θ` replaces `M` by

`M_θ=V_α(h_θ) M V_β(h_θ)^{-1}`

(up to the harmless inverse convention obtained by reversing whether the
automorphism or the state is rotated).

*Justification.*  The D2(b) intertwining relation on `A_α` and `A_β`, followed
by cancellation on every internal bond, is the same telescoping calculation
as Claim WI.  The outer environments are unchanged by Lemma IT(iv)
(`V_γ(h_θ)r_γV_γ(h_θ)^*=r_γ` and `l_γ=𝟙`), and D1(e′) fixes the remaining
junction contraction.  This is named calculation **FOL-ORB.1**.

**⟨2⟩2.**  `M_θ` is invertible, `M_0=M`, and `θ↦M_θ` is continuous near zero.

*Justification.*  Unitarity of the D2(b) matrices, `V_γ(e)=𝟙`, and the local
sections supplied by D2(e)(S), for `γ=α,β`.

**⟨2⟩3.**  Put

`ξ_θ := π(B(M_θ))Ω / ‖π(B(M_θ))Ω‖`.

Then `ξ_θ` is continuous, `ξ_0=Ω`, and its vector state is the rotated
`ϱ_0`.

*Justification.*  ⟨1⟩1 and ⟨2⟩1--⟨2⟩2; D1(a) supplies the GNS vector-state
convention.

**⟨2⟩4. QED.**

## 2. Spatial implementation and the Stone generator

In this section only, write `γ_θ` as a local abbreviation for the selected
circle automorphism of `𝔄`; no new global notation is introduced.

### ⟨1⟩3. LEMMA FOL-PURE (the fixed A2 kink is pure)

**ASSUME.**  `ϱ_0=ϱ_x^{(g)}` as in §0.

**PROVE.**  `ϱ_0` is pure, hence `π(𝔄)'=ℂ𝟙`.

**⟨2⟩1.**  Acting by `g` on every site of `[x,∞)` defines an automorphism
`η_g` of `𝔄`: on each local algebra it is a finite tensor product of adjoint
actions, and these compatible isometries extend from `𝔄_loc` by norm density.

*Justification.*  The quasi-local inductive-limit construction in D1(a) and
the half-string state action in D3(c); exact calculation **FOL-PURE.1**.

**⟨2⟩2.**  A2(b) identifies `ϱ_0` with `ω_α` composed with `η_g` (with the
inverse fixed by the D3(c) convention).  Since `ω_α` is pure and composition
with an automorphism preserves purity, `ϱ_0` is pure.

*Justification.*  Claim A2 and the purity statement in D1(d); if
`ω_α∘η_g=tσ_1+(1-t)σ_2`, composition with `η_g^{-1}` would give a nontrivial
convex decomposition of `ω_α` (**FOL-PURE.2**).

**⟨2⟩3.**  The GNS representation of a pure state is irreducible, so its
commutant is scalar.

*Justification.*  The GNS convention of D1(a) and the standard equivalence
“pure state iff irreducible GNS”, used here as exact functional-analytic fact
**FOL-PURE.3**.

**⟨2⟩4. QED.**

### ⟨1⟩4. LEMMA FOL-SPATIAL (a continuous projective implementer)

**ASSUME.**  ⟨1⟩2--⟨1⟩3.

**PROVE.**  There is a continuous homomorphism
`θ↦[W_θ]` from `ℝ` to the projective unitary group of `H` such that every
representative implements `γ_θ`; near zero its chosen representatives are
`C¹` on the dense local GNS domain.

**⟨2⟩1.**  For `θ` near zero choose the vector `ξ_{-θ}` of ⟨1⟩2 representing
`ϱ_0∘γ_{-θ}` and define on the dense GNS domain

`W_θ π(A)Ω := π(γ_θ(A))ξ_{-θ}`, `A∈𝔄`.

This rule is isometric.

*Justification.*  For `A,C∈𝔄`,

`⟨π(γ_θ(A))ξ_{-θ},π(γ_θ(C))ξ_{-θ}⟩`
`=(ϱ_0∘γ_{-θ})(γ_θ(A^*C))=ϱ_0(A^*C)`.

This is the GNS inner-product calculation **FOL-SP.1**, using D1(a) and
⟨1⟩2.

**⟨2⟩2.**  `W_θ` extends to a unitary and
`W_θπ(A)W_θ^*=π(γ_θ(A))`.

*Justification.*  A nonzero vector in the irreducible representation of
⟨1⟩3 is cyclic: the closure of `π(𝔄)ξ_{-θ}` is a nonzero invariant subspace.
Thus the range in ⟨2⟩1 is dense.  The displayed intertwining identity follows
on `π(𝔄)Ω` by multiplication.  Exact calculation **FOL-SP.2**, citing D1(a)
and ⟨1⟩3.

**⟨2⟩3.**  `θ↦W_θ` is strongly continuous near zero and is `C¹` on
`π(𝔄_loc)Ω`.

*Justification.*  For each `A∈𝔄`, `θ↦γ_θ(A)` is norm-continuous (first on
`𝔄_loc` by the finite-dimensional on-site circle and then on `𝔄` by D1(a)
norm density), while `θ↦ξ_{-θ}` is norm-continuous by ⟨1⟩2.  Hence the vectors
in ⟨2⟩1 vary continuously on a dense domain; the `W_θ` are unitaries, so the
continuity extends to all of `H`.  For `A∈𝔄_loc`, both factors are `C¹`:
the on-site action is finite-dimensional and `ξ_{-θ}` is `C¹` because D2(e)
is `C¹` and ⟨1⟩1.⟨2⟩3 is linear-affine.  Named estimate **FOL-SP.3**.

**⟨2⟩4.**  Any two unitaries implementing the same `γ_θ` differ by a scalar;
therefore, whenever `s,t,s+t` are in the local interval,

`[W_s][W_t]=[W_{s+t}]`.

*Justification.*  The quotient of two implementers commutes with `π(𝔄)` and
is scalar by ⟨1⟩3.  The group law `γ_sγ_t=γ_{s+t}` is the on-site circle law
of D2(a).  Named calculation **FOL-SP.4**.

**⟨2⟩5.**  The local projective homomorphism extends uniquely to every
`θ∈ℝ` by subdividing `θ` into sufficiently small equal increments.  It is
continuous and every class implements `γ_θ`.

*Justification.*  Independence of the subdivision follows from the local
law in ⟨2⟩4; products of the corresponding implementers implement products
of the D2(a) automorphisms.  This is the one-dimensional extension
calculation **FOL-SP.5**.

**⟨2⟩6. QED.**

### ⟨1⟩5. LEMMA FOL-LIFT (remove the local projective multiplier)

**ASSUME.**  A strongly continuous family `W_θ`, `|θ|<δ`, with `W_0=𝟙`,
which is `C¹` on a common dense invariant domain and satisfies the local
projective law `[W_s][W_t]=[W_{s+t}]` whenever `s,t,s+t∈(-δ,δ)`.

**PROVE.**  A scalar rephasing makes it an honest local unitary group, which
extends uniquely to a strongly continuous unitary group on `ℝ`.

**⟨2⟩1.**  On a smaller interval if necessary, the defect

`m(s,t)𝟙:=W_sW_tW_{s+t}^*`

is a `C¹`, normalised `U(1)`-valued two-cocycle and has a unique small
continuous real argument `f(s,t)` with `f(s,0)=f(0,t)=0`.

*Justification.*  The defect is scalar by the assumed projective law; it is
`C¹` by evaluation on any unit vector in the common domain, is near `1` by
strong continuity and `W_0=𝟙`, and associativity gives
`f(s,t)+f(s+t,u)=f(t,u)+f(s,t+u)` with no `2πℤ` defect after shrinking the
connected neighbourhood.  In the application the common domain and `C¹`
premise are supplied by D1(a), D2(e), and ⟨1⟩4.⟨2⟩3.  Exact calculation
**FOL-LIFT.1**.

**⟨2⟩2.**  Put `b(t):=∂_2f(t,0)` and
`a(t):=∫_0^t b(r)dr`.  Differentiating the cocycle identity in `u` at `u=0`
and then integrating in `t` gives

`∂_2f(s,t)=b(s+t)-b(t)`,
`f(s,t)=a(s+t)-a(s)-a(t)`.

*Justification.*  Direct differentiation of the identity in ⟨2⟩1 and the
fundamental theorem of calculus; exact calculation **FOL-LIFT.2**.  This is
where both the one-dimensional parameter and the D2(e) local `C¹` section are
used.

**⟨2⟩3.**  `U_0(θ):=e^{ia(θ)}W_θ` is strongly continuous and obeys
`U_0(s)U_0(t)=U_0(s+t)` whenever `s,t,s+t` lie in the smaller interval.

*Justification.*  Substitute the formula of ⟨2⟩2 into the defect in ⟨2⟩1;
named calculation **FOL-LIFT.3**, on the D1(a) GNS Hilbert space.

**⟨2⟩4.**  For arbitrary `t∈ℝ`, choose `n` with `|t/n|` in that interval and
define `U(t):=U_0(t/n)^n`.  This is independent of `n`, is a group
homomorphism, is strongly continuous, and has the same projective class as
the implementer of `γ_t`.

*Justification.*  Passing to a common refinement proves independence because
all partial sums representing one small increment stay in the local interval.
For `s,t`, choose a common sufficiently fine subdivision; the two small
unitaries commute by the local law and
`U(s)U(t)=U(s+t)`.  Continuity at zero is local, and continuity elsewhere
follows from the group law.  Products implement the corresponding products
of the D2(a) automorphisms.  Exact extension calculation **FOL-LIFT.4**.

**⟨2⟩5. QED.**

## 3. Why this does not revive M-INDEX-LA-strong

**ASSUME.**  The theorem ⟨1⟩6 and, when invoked, the nonscalar-tail
hypothesis of `theory/memory-index.md` ⟨1⟩12.

**PROVE.**  ⟨1⟩6 is compatible with the REFUTED claim
M-INDEX-LA-strong, including its on-folium mechanism B.

**⟨2⟩1.**  The unitaries in ⟨1⟩6 are obtained from folium equivalence:
locally, `W_θπ(A)Ω=π(γ_θ(A))ξ_{-θ}`, with `ξ_{-θ}` represented by one fixed

*Justification.*  ⟨1⟩1--⟨1⟩6; D1(a) identifies the fixed GNS folium.

**⟨2⟩2.**  If `V_{θ_0}` is nonscalar, the bare window unitaries
`e^{iθ_0Q̂_{W,c_0}}` are not Cauchy on `Ω`, even though `U(θ_0)` exists.

*Justification.*  Claim M-INDEX-LA-strong is REFUTED; its on-folium
strengthening is proved in `theory/memory-index.md` ⟨1⟩12 from the positive
shell limit `2(1-|tr(V_{θ_0}r)|²)`.  ⟨1⟩6 constructs `U` by a different
route.

**⟨2⟩3.**  There is no contradiction: an implementing unitary is determined
only up to a scalar, but it is not required to be the strong limit of any
chosen inner approximants.  Here the bare windows retain two virtual endpoint
excitations as their boundaries recede, whereas the GNS construction fixes
the automorphism directly and absorbs the rotated junction into `ξ_{-θ}`.

*Justification.*  Uniqueness modulo scalars is ⟨1⟩4.⟨2⟩4; failure of the
specific approximants is ⟨2⟩2; the finite-junction representative is
⟨1⟩1--⟨1⟩2.  This exact logical separation is named **FOL-FENCE.1**.

**⟨2⟩4.**  Mechanism A of M-INDEX-LA-strong remains outside the theorem:
its state has logarithmically divergent fluctuations and its circle orbit is
disjoint, while §0 fixes the single A2 representation whose circle orbit is
normal by ⟨1⟩2.

*Justification.*  Claim M-INDEX-LA-strong and
`theory/memory-index.md` ⟨1⟩3, versus ⟨1⟩2 here.

**⟨2⟩5. QED.**
