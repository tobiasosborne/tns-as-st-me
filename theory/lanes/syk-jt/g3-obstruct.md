# G3-OBSTRUCT: adversarial battery for the first non-Möbius charge

## ⟨1⟩0. Verdict, scope, and ranked attacks

⟨1⟩0.⟨2⟩1. **VERDICT.**  The negative branch of G3 is proved in two
important but delimited registers, and is **not** proved in the full
matter-coupled register posed by `corner-a-precision.md`.

1. There is no exact finite-\(N\) implementation of the literal primary
   rule (4.2) on a UV-complete, diagonal-stable bilocal core.  Nor can its
   Ward defect tend uniformly to zero on such a core.  The exact Majorana
   contact relation is the obstruction.
2. In pure JT, no nontrivial one-boundary derivation is spatially implementable
   by an operator affiliated with the PW boundary algebra.  That algebra is
   the abelian algebra generated by \(H\), so every affiliated unitary acts
   trivially by conjugation.
3. Any implementer of the \(n=2\) rule is necessarily nonconserved.  Exact
   conservation is incompatible with its nontrivial bracket with time
   translation.
4. A *bare* \(n=2\) mode fails diagonal-\(\widetilde{SL}(2,\mathbb R)\)
   descent.  This rules out promoting the unreduced Fourier mode itself; it
   does not rule out a matter-dressed or explicitly relational scalar.
5. On the conjectural LL type-\(\mathrm{III}_1\) factor, a covariant pair of
   spatial \(n=2\) modes closing on thermal translation is incompatible with
   modular outerness.  This is a conditional obstruction to the completed
   Witt package, not a single-mode G3 theorem.
6. Algebra type and energy positivity alone do not obstruct a single
   matter-coupled PW generator.  The type-\(\mathrm{II}_\infty\) factor has
   ample affiliated operators, and G3 does not require conservation.

The surviving object is therefore only a **UV-compatible soft tangent to
regulated bilocal moments**, defined in ⟨1⟩6.  It is kinematic, explicitly
broken, and not a charge.

⟨1⟩0.⟨2⟩2. **Operational register.**  Layer 0 is the parity-even finite-\(N\)
CAR algebra; Layer 1 consists of normalized bilocal sequences and their
finite products; the collective field and the reparametrization \(f\) are
not observables.  These are the binding declarations at
`theory/lanes/syk-jt/observables-spec.md:20-40,55-77`.  “Charge” below always
means the affiliated-generator definition and commutator register at
`theory/lanes/syk-jt/corner-a-precision.md:131-168`, not a saddle coordinate.

⟨1⟩0.⟨2⟩3. **Source facts used.**  The following are evidence, not silently
strengthened hypotheses.

* MSY describe full reparametrization as exact only in the perfect
  \(AdS_2\) limit and say that the nearly-\(AdS_2\) correction explicitly

  (`refs/arxiv-1606.01857/GravityReparametrizationsRevised.tex:234-265`).

## ⟨1⟩1. Rank 1 — the UV contact obstruction

### ⟨1⟩1.⟨2⟩1. THEOREM `G3-UV-NOEXACT`

Let

\[
 B_N(u,v):={1\over N}\sum_{i=1}^N\psi_i(u)\psi_i(v)
\tag{1.1}
\]

be a same-side, non-time-ordered bilocal in one finite-\(N\) SYK dot.  Write
\(\psi_i^2=c_\psi\mathbf 1\), where \(c_\psi>0\) (the convention in the
local SYK source has \(c_\psi=1/2\)).  Fix \(\Delta>0\) and a smooth real
\(\epsilon\) for which \(\epsilon'(u_0)\ne0\).  Suppose a unital
\(*\)-derivation \(d_N\) on the finite matrix algebra agrees, on every smooth
smearing of (1.1), with

\[
 T_{\epsilon,\Delta}B_N(u,v)=
 [\epsilon(u)\partial_u+\epsilon(v)\partial_v
   +\Delta\epsilon'(u)+\Delta\epsilon'(v)]B_N(u,v).
\tag{1.2}
\]

Suppose also that the smearing core is **diagonal-stable**: it contains a
smooth approximate identity \(\rho_k(u,v)\) concentrating at \((u_0,u_0)\),
and both the bilocal and its first derivatives may be passed through that
limit in operator norm.  Then no such \(d_N\) exists.  In particular no
self-adjoint \(Q_N\in\mathfrak A_N\) has
\(d_N=i[Q_N,\,\cdot\,]\) with (1.2) on this core.

⟨1⟩1.⟨2⟩2. **PROOF.**

⟨1⟩1.⟨2⟩2.⟨3⟩1.  Finite-dimensional Hamiltonian evolution is norm smooth,
and the CAR relation gives

\[
 B_N(u,u)=c_\psi\mathbf1,
 \qquad (\partial_u+\partial_v)B_N(u,v)|_{v=u}=0.
\tag{1.3}
\]

The source-side version of the first identity is the imposed limit
\(G(\tau)\to\tfrac12\operatorname{sgn}\tau\)
(`refs/arxiv-2412.14799/Main.tex:117-122`).

⟨1⟩1.⟨2⟩2.⟨3⟩2.  By (1.2)--(1.3) and diagonal stability,

\[
 \lim_{k\to\infty}T_{\epsilon,\Delta}B_N(\rho_k)
 =2\Delta\epsilon'(u_0)c_\psi\mathbf1.
\tag{1.4}
\]

⟨1⟩1.⟨2⟩2.⟨3⟩3.  On the other hand,
\(B_N(\rho_k)\to c_\psi\mathbf1\) in norm.  Every derivation annihilates the
unit, and every derivation of a finite-dimensional C*-algebra is bounded.
Therefore

\[
 \lim_{k\to\infty}d_N(B_N(\rho_k))
 =d_N(c_\psi\mathbf1)=0.
\tag{1.5}
\]

Equations (1.4) and (1.5) contradict \(\Delta>0\), \(c_\psi>0\), and
\(\epsilon'(u_0)\ne0\).  QED.

⟨1⟩1.⟨2⟩3. **Specialization to G3.**  For
\(\epsilon_{2,c}(u)=\cos 2u\), choose \(u_0=\pi/4\).  Then
\(\epsilon'_{2,c}(u_0)=-2\), so the nonzero contact residual in (1.4) is
\(-4\Delta c_\psi\mathbf1\) (\(-2\Delta\mathbf1\) in the convention
\(c_\psi=1/2\)).  This is an operator-relation obstruction, not a failure to
guess the right \(Q_N\).

⟨1⟩1.⟨2⟩4. **Uniform-defect corollary.**  Let \(d_N=i[Q_N,\cdot]\), and
suppose a Ward estimate is required on all \(B_N(\rho_k)\) with one constant
independent of both \(N\) and the diagonal-localization index \(k\).  Taking
\(k\to\infty\) first in any unit vector gives a defect at least
\(2\Delta c_\psi|\epsilon'(u_0)|\).  Hence no \(r_N\to0\) can make that
estimate true.  This is the precise sense in which the full UV core has an
uncontrolled G6 defect.

⟨1⟩1.⟨2⟩5. **Exact scope fence.**  `G3-UV-NOEXACT` rules out:

* exact Layer-0 primary action on all same-side bilocals, including their
  coincident-time norm closure;
* a Ward bound uniform over a diagonal-localizing family; and
* interpreting the strict conformal rule itself as a microscopic CAR
  automorphism.

It does **not** rule out:

* convergence for each fixed test function whose support stays a positive
  distance from the diagonal;
* constants \(C_{A,\delta}\) that diverge as the smearing approaches the
  UV strip;
* a rule corrected by contact/UV terms; or
* a derivation only on the emergent distributional algebra, where the
  coincident product is deliberately absent.

This fence matters because G3 allows an \(A\)-dependent constant and fixes
\(\delta>0\).  The theorem therefore defeats the strongest “full operational
core” reading but does not by itself refute G3 on a fixed separated core.

## ⟨1⟩2. Rank 2 — pure JT has no room for a spatial action

### ⟨1⟩2.⟨2⟩1. THEOREM `G3-PURE-JT-TRIVIAL`

In pure JT, let \(Q=Q^*\) be affiliated with either PW boundary algebra
\(\mathcal A_R^{\rm JT}\), \(\mathcal A_L^{\rm JT}\), or their join.  Then

\[
 \operatorname{Ad}(e^{isQ})(A)=A
 \quad\hbox{for every }A\hbox{ in that algebra and every }s\in\mathbb R.
\tag{2.1}
\]

Consequently no such \(Q\) implements a nonzero bilocal derivation.

⟨1⟩2.⟨2⟩2. **PROOF.**

⟨1⟩2.⟨2⟩2.⟨3⟩1.  PW prove that in pure JT

\[
 \mathcal A_R^{\rm JT}=\mathcal A_L^{\rm JT}=W^*(H),
\tag{2.2}
\]

and that this is a commutative algebra; see
`refs/arxiv-2301.07257/Paper.tex:751-769`.  Its two-sided join is therefore
the same algebra.

⟨1⟩2.⟨2⟩2.⟨3⟩2.  Affiliation means that every spectral projection of \(Q\)
lies in \(W^*(H)\), equivalently \(e^{isQ}\in W^*(H)\) for all real \(s\).
Since (2.2) is abelian, \(e^{isQ}\) commutes with every \(A\in W^*(H)\).
This proves (2.1).  Differentiating on any commutator core gives the zero
derivation.  QED.

⟨1⟩2.⟨2⟩3. **Scope.**  The theorem rules out both one-boundary and two-sided
pure-JT non-Möbius charges, exact or kinematic, whenever “charge” means an
affiliated spatial implementer.  It does not use energy positivity; it uses
the complete PW invariant-algebra theorem.  It also does not act on a matter
bilocal, because pure JT has none.  Adding matter changes (2.2) to a
noncommutative type-\(\mathrm{II}_\infty\) factor generated by \(H_X\) and
smeared \(\Phi_X(t)\)
(`refs/arxiv-2301.07257/Paper.tex:817-852`).  Thus matter opens algebraic room;
PW do not prove that a particular \(n=2\) implementer occupies it.

## ⟨1⟩3. Rank 3 — an \(n=2\) implementer cannot be exactly conserved

### ⟨1⟩3.⟨2⟩1. THEOREM `G3-NOT-CONSERVED`

Let \(H_X\) generate physical one-boundary time translations on a
time-translation-invariant bilocal commutator core \(\mathcal D\).  Suppose a
self-adjoint \(Q_{2,c}\) implements (4.2) for
\(\epsilon_{2,c}=\cos 2u\) on \(\mathcal D\), and suppose all Jacobi
commutators below exist on one invariant Hilbert-space core.  If the action is
nonzero, then \([H_X,Q_{2,c}]\ne0\) on that core.  The same conclusion holds
for a two-sided generator with the corresponding common time-translation
Hamiltonian.

⟨1⟩3.⟨2⟩2. **PROOF.**

⟨1⟩3.⟨2⟩2.⟨3⟩1.  Write \(\delta_1\) for simultaneous translation of the
two bilocal arguments.  PW give
\(\Phi_X(t)=e^{itH_X}\Phi_X(0)e^{-itH_X}\) at
`refs/arxiv-2301.07257/Paper.tex:817-826`, so the commutator derivation of
\(H_X\) is \(\delta_1\) on \(\mathcal D\).

⟨1⟩3.⟨2⟩2.⟨3⟩2.  Primary Lie derivatives obey the vector-field identity

\[
 [\delta_1,\delta_\epsilon]
 =\delta_{\epsilon'},
\tag{3.1}
\]

by direct expansion of (4.2), including its weight terms.  For
\(\epsilon=\cos2u\), the right side is
\(-2\delta_{\sin2u}\), which is nonzero on a nontrivial primary bilocal.

⟨1⟩3.⟨2⟩2.⟨3⟩3.  If \([H_X,Q_{2,c}]=0\), the Jacobi identity makes their
commutator derivations commute.  That contradicts (3.1).  QED.

⟨1⟩3.⟨2⟩3. **Scope.**  This theorem does not deny a kinematic
\(Q_{2,c}\), nor an approximately conserved one with an explicit breaking
insertion.  It proves that “exactly conserved non-Möbius charge” is the wrong
status even if spatial implementation is eventually constructed.  The
breaking is not optional: MSY call reparametrization both spontaneous and
explicitly broken (`refs/arxiv-1606.01857/GravityReparametrizationsRevised.tex:234-265`),
and Bucca--Mezei identify the discarded \(\partial_\tau\) term as the explicit
breaking (`refs/arxiv-2412.14799/Main.tex:124-146`).

## ⟨1⟩4. Rank 4 — the bare mode does not descend through the constraint

### ⟨1⟩4.⟨2⟩1. PROPOSITION `G3-BARE-DESCENT`

Let \(L_{-1},L_0,L_1\) denote the complexified diagonal
\(\mathfrak{sl}_2\) constraints on the unreduced Hilbert space, and let
\(X_2\) be a bare Witt mode with

\[
 [L_0,X_2]=-2X_2.
\tag{4.1}
\]

If physical vectors obey \(L_a\Psi=0\), then \(X_2\) cannot map the physical
domain into itself unless it acts there as zero.

⟨1⟩4.⟨2⟩2. **PROOF.**  If \(X_2\Psi\) were physical, then

\[
 0=L_0X_2\Psi=[L_0,X_2]\Psi+X_2L_0\Psi=-2X_2\Psi.
\tag{4.2}
\]

Hence \(X_2\Psi=0\).  The same proof applies to \(X_{-2}\); real cosine and
sine modes are their real linear combinations.  PW's actual constraint is
\(J_a^L+J_a^R\), or \(J_a^L+J_a^R+J_a^{\rm matt}\) with matter
(`refs/arxiv-2301.07257/Paper.tex:481-515,632-660`), and the total constraint
annihilates physical coinvariants
(`refs/arxiv-2301.07257/Paper.tex:602-610`).  QED.

⟨1⟩4.⟨2⟩3. **Scope and failed strengthening.**  The proposition kills the
bare Fourier generator on the unreduced boundary/soft coordinate.  It does
not kill an operator dressed with gravitational or matter reference data so
that it commutes with all three total constraints.  Indeed PW's dressed
one-boundary matter insertions are gauge invariant even though bare matter
positions are not (`refs/arxiv-2301.07257/Paper.tex:676-680,817-836`).  Thus
diagonal \(SL(2)\) does not prove an outright one-boundary no-go once matter
is present.  A two-sided relational dressing is another open escape route.

## ⟨1⟩5. Rank 5 — modular obstruction to a completed inner mode pair

### ⟨1⟩5.⟨2⟩1. CONDITIONAL THEOREM `G3-LL-NOCLOSURE`

Assume all of the following.

1. The LL one-sided algebra \(\mathcal M_R^{\rm LL}\) is a
   type-\(\mathrm{III}_1\) factor, as LL conjecture rather than prove at
   `refs/arxiv-2110.05497/causal_connectability_PRD.tex:663-697`.
2. Its faithful thermal modular group \(\sigma_s\) is boundary time
   translation, as identified at
   `refs/arxiv-2110.05497/causal_connectability_PRD.tex:887-891`.
3. An affiliated \(Q_{2,c}\) implements \(\delta_{\cos2\tau}\), its modular
   translates preserve one common analytic operator/Hilbert core, and the
   transported cosine and sine derivations close and exponentiate according
   to their vector-field bracket; specifically, the corresponding
   BCH/Trotter commutator products converge strongly to a unitary in
   \(\mathcal M_R^{\rm LL}\) implementing the bracket flow.

Then these assumptions are inconsistent.  In particular, the LL factor
cannot contain a covariant spatial \(n=2\) cosine/sine pair whose bracket
exponentiates to thermal translation.

⟨1⟩5.⟨2⟩2. **PROOF.**

⟨1⟩5.⟨2⟩2.⟨3⟩1.  If \(Q_{2,c}\) is affiliated with
\(\mathcal M_R^{\rm LL}\), then \(e^{irQ_{2,c}}\in\mathcal M_R^{\rm LL}\).
Applying the automorphism \(\sigma_s\) gives another unitary in the same
factor and transports its action to the time-translated vector field.  A
quarter-period translation therefore supplies the sine mode under assumption
3.

⟨1⟩5.⟨2⟩2.⟨3⟩2.  Direct calculation gives

\[
 [\cos2\tau,\sin2\tau]_{\rm vf}=2,
 \qquad
 [\delta_{\cos2\tau},\delta_{\sin2\tau}]=2\delta_1.
\tag{5.1}
\]

By the strong BCH/Trotter clause in assumption 3, the group generated from
the two inner mode actions therefore supplies a unitary in the factor that
implements a nonzero thermal translation.

⟨1⟩5.⟨2⟩2.⟨3⟩3.  The Connes \(T\)-invariant of a type-\(\mathrm{III}_1\)
factor is \(T(\mathcal M)=\{0\}\): no nonzero member of the faithful modular
group is inner.  This contradicts ⟨3⟩2.  QED.

⟨1⟩5.⟨2⟩3. **Why this does not settle G3.**  This theorem uses the standard
type-\(\mathrm{III}_1\) modular-outerness theorem in addition to the quoted
local sources.  More importantly, G3 asks initially for only one cosine mode
and does not assume the common analytic closure or exponentiation in item 3;
those are essentially G4 hypotheses.  Dropping them blocks the argument at
⟨3⟩2.  The theorem is therefore a sharp obstruction to advertising a full
inner Diff/Witt package on the LL factor, not a proof that one closable
cosine derivation is outer.

⟨1⟩5.⟨2⟩4. **PW comparison.**  The contradiction is absent in PW's
type-\(\mathrm{II}_\infty\) algebra because \(H_R\) is itself affiliated and
its time translation is inner.  Algebra type alone consequently supplies no
PW no-go.  Nor does positivity: PW prove \(H_R,H_L\ge0\)
(`refs/arxiv-2301.07257/Paper.tex:662-673`), but G3 does not demand that
\(Q_{2,c}\) be semibounded or conserved.  **The energy-positivity attack
fails for a single kinematic mode.**

## ⟨1⟩6. Explicit breaking, the failed universal Ward attack, and the survivor

⟨1⟩6.⟨2⟩1. **What the microscopic source actually proves.**  Bucca--Mezei's
large-\(N\) collective action starts from
\(G=(1/N)\sum_iT\psi_i\psi_i\) and the exact Schwinger--Dyson equation
containing \(\partial_\tau\)
(`refs/arxiv-2412.14799/Main.tex:97-115`).  They obtain the conformal
reparametrization rule only after dropping \(\partial_\tau\), and say that
restoring it explicitly breaks the rule
(`refs/arxiv-2412.14799/Main.tex:124-146`).  In the large-\(p\) variables the
same obstruction is the exact boundary condition

\[
 g(\tau,\tau)=0,
\tag{6.1}
\]

together with symmetry and KMS; see
`refs/arxiv-2412.14799/Main.tex:149-161`.  The conformal transformation of
the Liouville field violates this boundary condition at finite coupling, and
the symmetry returns only at strong coupling
(`refs/arxiv-2412.14799/Main.tex:223-253,313-335`).  This is the collective
field version of `G3-UV-NOEXACT`.

In the controlled large-\(p\) BCFT description, the leading breaking is the
boundary displacement insertion
\(S_{\rm ZZ}-\pi\delta v\int d\bar\tau\,\widehat D(\bar\tau)+\cdots\), whose
evaluation on reparametrized saddles gives the Schwarzian and lifts them at
order \(\delta v\)
(`refs/arxiv-2412.14799/Main.tex:280-311`).  This identifies a collective
breaking term, not a parity-even finite-\(N\) operator
\(\mathcal B_N[\epsilon]\) with a G6 matrix-element bound.

⟨1⟩6.⟨2⟩2. **Why route (b) does not prove its universal quantifier.**  The
sources derive an effective action and a soft saddle; they do not construct
parity-even \(Q_N\), identify an operator \(\mathcal B_N[\epsilon]\), or bound

\[
 \langle\Psi_N,(i[H_N,Q_N]-\mathcal B_N)\Psi_N\rangle
\tag{6.2}
\]

uniformly on the G6 vector family.  At general \(p\), the UV/IR matching even
requires numerical input for the Schwarzian coefficient
(`refs/arxiv-2412.14799/Main.tex:59-70`).  Their controlled large-\(p\) order
is \(N\to\infty\) first and \(p\to\infty\) second
(`refs/arxiv-2412.14799/Main.tex:149-150`).  Absence of a bound in these
sources is not a proof that *every possible* approximant fails.  Route (b) is
proved only for a diagonal-uniform core by ⟨1⟩1; it remains open for every
fixed separated smearing with its own \(C_{A,\delta}\).

### ⟨1⟩6.⟨2⟩3. DEFINITION `G3-UV-SOFT-TANGENT` (surviving weaker object)

Let \(v<1\) be Bucca--Mezei's finite-coupling saddle parameter, define
\(\delta v:=1-v\) (their strong-coupling convention at
`refs/arxiv-2412.14799/Main.tex:163-180`), and set

\[
 f_s(\tau)=\tau+s\epsilon_{2,c}(\tau),\qquad
 \epsilon_{2,c}(\tau)=\cos2\tau.
\tag{6.3}
\]

For \(\tau_1\ne\tau_2\), define the UV-compatible saddle embedding

\[
 e^{\gamma_{f_s}(\tau_1,\tau_2)}:=e^{\gamma_*(\tau_1,\tau_2)}
 {\sin^2\!\frac{\tau_2-\tau_1}{2}
  \over
  \sin^2\!\frac{f_s(\tau_2)-f_s(\tau_1)}{2}}
 f_s'(\tau_1)f_s'(\tau_2),
\tag{6.4}
\]

and define the **UV-compatible soft tangent** by

\[
 \mathsf V^{(v)}_{2,c}\gamma_*:=
 {d\over ds}\gamma_{f_s}\big|_{s=0}
 =\epsilon'_{2,c}(\tau_1)+\epsilon'_{2,c}(\tau_2)
 -(\epsilon_{2,c}(\tau_2)-\epsilon_{2,c}(\tau_1))
       \cot{\tau_2-\tau_1\over2}.
\tag{6.5}
\]

Equation (6.4) is exactly the source's boundary-condition-preserving Ansatz
(`refs/arxiv-2412.14799/Main.tex:327-348`).  Thus the definition is not a new
microscopic construction.

⟨1⟩6.⟨2⟩4. **PROVE (contact compatibility).**  Put
\(\bar\tau=(\tau_1+\tau_2)/2\) and \(d=\tau_1-\tau_2\).  Elementary
trigonometry reduces (6.5) to

\[
 \mathsf V^{(v)}_{2,c}\gamma_*
 =2\sin(2\bar\tau)(1-\cos d)=O(d^2).
\tag{6.6}
\]

It therefore preserves (6.1), unlike applying a regular primary rule through
the finite-\(N\) contact point.  Equivalently, the source's linear mode has
radial profile

\[
 f_2(d)={\sin d\over\tan(d/2)}-2\cos d=1-\cos d,
\tag{6.7}
\]

from `refs/arxiv-2412.14799/Main.tex:411-416`.  QED.

⟨1⟩6.⟨2⟩5. **Operational meaning and limit order.**  For a fixed
\(\ell>0\), smear the tangent only with Layer-1 test functions supported in
\(|\tau_1-\tau_2|\ge\ell\) (circle distance understood).  The source
definition
\(G=\tfrac12\operatorname{sgn}(\tau_1-\tau_2)(1+g/p)\), with \(\gamma\)
differing from \(g\) by an \(f\)-independent constant, gives the raw
Layer-1 response

\[
 {dG_{f_s}(\tau_1,\tau_2)\over ds}\bigg|_{s=0}
 ={\operatorname{sgn}(\tau_1-\tau_2)\over2p}
   \mathsf V^{(v)}_{2,c}\gamma_*+O(p^{-2}),
\tag{6.8}
\]

using `refs/arxiv-2412.14799/Main.tex:149-178`.  The controlled reading is:
first \(N\to\infty\); then use the large-\(p\) expansion at a declared large
but finite \(p\); then take \(\delta v\to0\) with
\(\delta v/\ell\to0\).  No rescaling of the detector by \(p\) is part of the
observable.  In the strict subsequent \(p\to\infty\) limit the raw response
in (6.8) vanishes.

In that register, (6.8) is a tangent to predictions for allowed bilocal
moments.  Bucca--Mezei state that the Ansatz realizes the infinitesimal
reparametrization at linear order, while its possible nonlinear mixing with
hard directions is not completely established
(`refs/arxiv-2412.14799/Main.tex:411-425`).  The collective field \(f\) is
GAUGE/EFT bookkeeping; only the changed bilocal statistics are Layer-1 data.

⟨1⟩6.⟨2⟩6. **Status fence for the survivor.**  `G3-UV-SOFT-TANGENT` is:

* **operational only through the resulting one-sided bilocal statistics**;
  no frame label is declared observable, and no two-sided relational
  extension is asserted;
* **approximately conformal only on separated IR smearings** in (6.8);
* **kinematic and explicitly broken**, not conserved; and
* **not a generator**: no self-adjoint affiliated \(Q\), no parity-even
  \(Q_N\), no common commutator core, and no \(r_N\) Ward estimate has been
  obtained.

It is the strongest weaker object that survives every attack in this lane.
