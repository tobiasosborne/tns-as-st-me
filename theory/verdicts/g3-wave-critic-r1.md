## 1. Independent reconstruction of the binding target

For \(\epsilon_{2,c}(\tau)=\cos 2\tau\), the formal primary-bilocal rule is

\[
 \delta_2 B_\Delta(u,v)=
 \left[\cos 2u\,\partial_u+\cos 2v\,\partial_v
 -2\Delta\sin 2u-2\Delta\sin 2v\right]B_\Delta(u,v).
\tag{1.1}
\]

G3 asks for much more than (1.1): descent through physical operator
relations; a self-adjoint spatial implementer affiliated with a named

dense invariant commutator core; parity-even finite-\(N\) approximants; and a
proved rate tending to zero that controls both finite size and finite

The allowed operational registers are Layer-0 parity-even finite-\(N\) CAR
observables and Layer-1 limits of their bilocal statistics.  The collective

statement below is therefore phrased either as an affiliated implementer or
as a response of allowed bilocal expectations; no state distinction is
assigned to an unmeasured label.

## 2. Obstruct lane: theorem-strength recomputation

### 2.1 `G3-UV-NOEXACT`

Let

\[
 B_N(u,v)=N^{-1}\sum_i\psi_i(u)\psi_i(v),\qquad
 \psi_i^2=c_\psi\mathbf 1.
\]

At finite \(N\), norm-smooth Hamiltonian evolution and the CAR give

\[
 B_N(u,u)=c_\psi\mathbf1,qquad
 (\partial_u+\partial_v)B_N(u,v)|_{v=u}=0.
\tag{2.1}
\]

Applying the literal primary rule to a norm approximate identity localized
at \((u_0,u_0)\) therefore tends to

\[
 2\Delta\epsilon'(u_0)c_\psi\mathbf1,
\tag{2.2}
\]

whereas any derivation of the finite matrix algebra sends the same limit to
zero because it annihilates the unit and is bounded.  For
\(\epsilon=\cos2u\), \(u_0=\pi/4\), \(\Delta=1/4\), and
\(c_\psi=1/2\), the absolute residual is \(1/2\).  The calculation is
correct.

The source attribution is also real: Bucca--Mezei define the bilocal and
impose \(G(\tau)\to\tfrac12\operatorname{sgn}\tau\) from the Majorana CAR at
`refs/arxiv-2412.14799/Main.tex:97-122`; they obtain reparametrization
invariance only after dropping the microscopic derivative and say that
restoring it explicitly breaks the symmetry at `Main.tex:124-146`.

The quantifier fence is essential.  This proves no exact literal primary
action on a diagonal-stable same-side UV core and no vanishing estimate
uniform over a diagonal-localizing family.  It does **not** address each
fixed separated smearing with its own \(C_{A,\delta}\), a contact-corrected
rule, an emergent distributional core without the diagonal, or the
matter-dressed relational route.  It therefore does not alone refute G3 as
posed at fixed regulator.

**Ruling: PROMOTE with its existing scope fence; HOLD any use as a no-go for
the fixed separated G3 core.**

### 2.2 `G3-PURE-JT-TRIVIAL`

PW state that the pure-JT left and right boundary algebras are equal,
commutative, and generated only by \(H=H_L=H_R\) at
`refs/arxiv-2301.07257/Paper.tex:751-769`.  Hence an affiliated self-adjoint
\(Q\) has \(e^{isQ}\in W^*(H)\); this unitary commutes with every element of
that algebra, and its adjoint derivation is zero.  Because the two pure-JT
algebras coincide, their join does not enlarge the result.

This is a valid one-boundary and two-sided pure-JT theorem, but pure JT has no
matter bilocal.  PW's matter-coupled algebra is instead generated by bounded

**Ruling: PROMOTE for pure JT only.**

### 2.3 `G3-NOT-CONSERVED`

`refs/arxiv-2301.07257/Paper.tex:817-826`.  Direct expansion of the primary

\[
 [\delta_1,\delta_\epsilon]=\delta_{\epsilon'}.
\tag{2.3}

(`refs/arxiv-1606.01857/GravityReparametrizationsRevised.tex:1015-1018`).

## 3. The surviving soft tangent

Differentiate the Bucca--Mezei boundary-condition-preserving Ansatz
`refs/arxiv-2412.14799/Main.tex:327-348` along
\(f_s(\tau)=\tau+s\cos2\tau\).  Direct differentiation gives

\[
 \mathsf V_{2,c}\gamma_*=\epsilon'_2(\tau_1)+\epsilon'_2(\tau_2)
 -(\epsilon_2(\tau_2)-\epsilon_2(\tau_1))
 \cot\frac{\tau_2-\tau_1}{2}.
\tag{3.1}
\]

Writing \(\bar\tau=(\tau_1+\tau_2)/2\) and
\(d=\tau_1-\tau_2\), independent trigonometric reduction yields

\[
 \mathsf V_{2,c}\gamma_*=2\sin(2\bar\tau)(1-\cos d)=O(d^2).
\tag{3.2}
\]

The source's linear radial profile gives the same result:
\(\sin d/\tan(d/2)-2\cos d=1-\cos d\)
(`Main.tex:411-416`).  Thus the tangent preserves the collective contact
boundary condition.  From
\(G=\tfrac12\operatorname{sgn}(d)(1+g/p)\) and the \(f\)-independence of the
constant relating \(g\) and \(\gamma\), its raw response is

\[
 \frac{dG_{f_s}}{ds}\bigg|_{s=0}
 =\frac{\operatorname{sgn}(d)}{2p}\mathsf V_{2,c}\gamma_*+O(p^{-2}).
\tag{3.3}
\]

This object is well-defined as a tangent to separated Euclidean bilocal
expectations in the source's order: first \(N\to\infty\), then the large-\(p\)
expansion, then strong coupling with \(\delta v/\ell\to0\) for support
\(|d|\ge\ell>0\).  Without detector rescaling its raw response vanishes in a
subsequent strict \(p\to\infty\) limit.  It has no affiliated self-adjoint
generator, finite-\(N\) approximant, invariant commutator core, or Ward rate.

### 4.3 The two lanes' “kinematic” objects are not identical

regulated bilocal word algebra**, after choosing a Lorentzian continuation.
