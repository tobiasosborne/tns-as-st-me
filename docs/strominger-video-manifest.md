non-YouTube venues (PIRSA, Cornell VOD, ICTP-SAIFR). Strominger as **speaker**
only — items where he is merely mentioned, or where another physicist speaks
about related material, are excluded from the table (a partial exclusion list
is noted at the end for transparency). Re-uploads/duplicate recordings of the
same talk are flagged in the Note column rather than silently dropped.

`yt-dlp --list-subs`); Unknown = not individually checked (search-result
listing only) — treat as likely Y given YouTube's near-universal ASR coverage,
