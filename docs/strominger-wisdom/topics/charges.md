## The antipodal matching condition

- **There is no first-principles proof, as far as he knows.** It is an observed property of a 130-year-old formula, verified in every example, and derivable from the quantum soft photon theorem — which he finds backwards: "it seems pretty weird to be using quantum field theory to prove results relevant to 19th-century electromagnetism … I would think that there would be some set of assumptions and some way to prove it, make it into a mathematical theorem, but I haven't seen that." `[lvyyxkjwSCg @ 01:26:02]` — [L1](../lectures/01-lvyyxkjwSCg-digest.md)
- **His hedged bet, and why the condition cannot simply be dropped:** it is "probably not hard to prove" that antipodal matching is the only Lorentz-invariant boundary condition, though he has not tried; and some such condition is mandatory, because otherwise the scattering problem is not defined. `[lvyyxkjwSCg @ 01:28:42]` — [L1](../lectures/01-lvyyxkjwSCg-digest.md)
- **TENSION — a year earlier he stated it more strongly:** "as far as I know there's only one Lorentz-invariant CPT-invariant way of specifying that boundary condition … and moreover the agreement with the soft graviton theorem implies that that boundary condition is the one that's implicitly being used when we do Feynman diagrams". `[CdFyoaNv8GI @ 00:57:05]` — [extra-01](../lectures/extra-01-CdFyoaNv8GI-digest.md). Keep both: the 2015 claim is uniqueness asserted, the 2016 claim is uniqueness conjectured and untried.
- **And the third register, from the last lecture: it is an assumption, and without it there is no theory.** "If we don't assume there's some kind of matching conditions … we just don't have a theory. What you're asking me is how do we know that there is a theory." `[H4IZ3jwTDp4 @ 00:28:37]` — [L10](../lectures/10-H4IZ3jwTDp4-digest.md)
- **…with the physical plausibility argument that precedes it:** the far field does not know how complicated the near field was — "the long distance metric from the Sun is no different than the long distance metric from a Schwarzschild". `[H4IZ3jwTDp4 @ 00:28:37]` — [L10](../lectures/10-H4IZ3jwTDp4-digest.md)
- **He prefers the phrasing "the fields are constant along the generators of null infinity, including where they cross spatial infinity"**, because "same value at the two ends of a light ray" will not survive curvature and caustics in quantum gravity. The condition surprised him at first and has grown more natural with use. `[lvyyxkjwSCg @ 01:33:58]` — [L1](../lectures/01-lvyyxkjwSCg-digest.md)
- **A student's picture, endorsed:** a free particle flying through the middle enters at one angle and leaves at the opposite one. `[yeMZKViadhw @ 01:12:34]` — [L2](../lectures/02-yeMZKViadhw-digest.md)
- **The condition is implicit in Feynman propagators, which is how half a century of non-abelian gauge theory got away without stating it.** `[OXl7IXT7_Cw @ 00:52:56]` — [L6](../lectures/06-OXl7IXT7_Cw-digest.md)
- **EARLY PERIOD (2013): none of this.** Scri⁻ is explicitly set aside as a nuisance ("there's an obstruction … I want to ignore scri minus"), and the antipodal identification does not appear at all. `[7S3Eg4VlZf0 @ 00:17:01]` — [extra-03](../lectures/extra-03-7S3Eg4VlZf0-digest.md)

## The discontinuity everything rests on

- **He had seen and suppressed the Liénard–Wiechert discontinuity for his whole career and only noticed it about a year before the course** — he calls it "the bedrock of everything we do from here on" and goes through it in deliberately pedantic detail for that reason. `[lvyyxkjwSCg @ 00:35:17]` — [L1](../lectures/01-lvyyxkjwSCg-digest.md)
- **The same admission twice more:** "I'm sure that Maxwell and Liénard and Wiechert knew this, but I only became fully aware of it myself a year or two ago" `[wg-4Rm-kk70 @ 00:05:28]` — [extra-02](../lectures/extra-02-wg-4Rm-kk70-digest.md); and "I didn't realize until the last few years … this fact is going to be crucial to everything I'm going to say, so I'm going to belabor it a little bit" `[F42XbX45Dzw @ 00:11:16]` — [extra-05](../lectures/extra-05-F42XbX45Dzw-digest.md)

## Constructing charges

- **BMS never managed to construct the charges, and rival candidate groups (Newman–Unti among them) could not be excluded without more work.** `[swrNwD0ypaE @ 00:31:57]` — [L3](../lectures/03-swrNwD0ypaE-digest.md)
- **Forty years separated the BMS algebra from the BMS charges** — "they're not the same thing, it's very hard to go from one to the other". `[7S3Eg4VlZf0 @ 00:42:39]` — [extra-03](../lectures/extra-03-7S3Eg4VlZf0-digest.md)
- **A vanishing charge does not reliably mean the transformation is trivial**, so the natural fix to the "allowed/trivial" definition does not always work. `[swrNwD0ypaE @ 00:13:01]` — [L3](../lectures/03-swrNwD0ypaE-digest.md)
- **The magnetic case has an unsolved canonical problem** — the Dirac brackets with magnetic charge present have not been computed, so the statement about which symmetry the magnetic charges generate is an educated guess from duality covariance rather than a derivation. He is explicit about the difference, and says his group is working on it. `[xZ2FG1kLb9I @ 01:44:19]` — [L4](../lectures/04-xZ2FG1kLb9I-digest.md)
- **The photino charge has a wrinkle:** the field components diverge linearly in `u` at the edges of scri, and a delicate projection is needed — "that's an instructive exercise but it takes half an hour not three minutes". `[WxCY-K-Jb4A @ 01:41:13]`, `[WxCY-K-Jb4A @ 01:42:17]`, `[WxCY-K-Jb4A @ 01:43:19]` — [L5](../lectures/05-WxCY-K-Jb4A-digest.md); called "ubiquitous" and traced back to problem set 1 at `[60U7_P2XJlQ @ 00:59:29]` — [L8](../lectures/08-60U7_P2XJlQ-digest.md)
- **He is deliberate about how the slice is terminated near timelike infinity, and says the limit is where the simplicity comes from.** `[xZ2FG1kLb9I @ 00:56:41]` — [L4](../lectures/04-xZ2FG1kLb9I-digest.md)
- **The route he did not take, and why:** matching classically is harder because you are subtracting a finite piece off a `u`-divergence and the ambiguities have to be controlled. `[60U7_P2XJlQ @ 01:07:02]` — [L8](../lectures/08-60U7_P2XJlQ-digest.md)
- **Charges can be defined on intermediate slices too — he does not know whether anything is to be learned from it**, though the black-hole work did something of the kind. `[yeMZKViadhw @ 00:35:19]` — [L2](../lectures/02-yeMZKViadhw-digest.md)
- **The news–`C` commutator is derived, not assumed** — "and there's also what else could it be". `[60U7_P2XJlQ @ 00:55:07]`, with the free-field argument at `[60U7_P2XJlQ @ 00:52:00]` — [L8](../lectures/08-60U7_P2XJlQ-digest.md)
- **Why pushing the Cauchy surface up to null infinity is legitimate:** the symplectic form does not depend on the slice ("there's a million papers on that"), and at scri everything has dispersed and become weak, so the theory there is essentially free — even though the resulting commutators look nothing like the textbook ones. `[yeMZKViadhw @ 01:35:16]` — [L2](../lectures/02-yeMZKViadhw-digest.md)
- **EARLY PERIOD (2013): he was deliberately *not* constructing global charges**, on the grounds that they do not act naturally on `R³` slices with `S²` boundaries and that a Kac–Moody central term needs a circle. The mature programme does exactly what he here declines to do. `[7S3Eg4VlZf0 @ 00:43:42]` — [extra-03](../lectures/extra-03-7S3Eg4VlZf0-digest.md)

## What the charges say

- **Energy conservation at every angle**, with the caveat about the linear total-derivative term attached in the same sentence rather than buried. `[6kH2pl_zvMA @ 00:22:05]` — [cstq](../lectures/cstq-6kH2pl_zvMA-digest.md)
- **The paradox he cheerfully accepts:** soft quanta carry no energy yet redistribute it — because "energy at an angle" is a field quantity (the radial component of the electric field), not a particle count. `[CdFyoaNv8GI @ 00:52:50]`, `[CdFyoaNv8GI @ 00:51:49]` — [extra-01](../lectures/extra-01-CdFyoaNv8GI-digest.md)
- **…and a concession on that very point:** pressed that in a `U(1)` theory the radial electric field and the charge current are both separately gauge invariant, he gives ground on his uniqueness claim. `[CdFyoaNv8GI @ 00:52:50]` — [extra-01](../lectures/extra-01-CdFyoaNv8GI-digest.md)
- **The Bondi mass rises along scri⁻ to its maximum, the ADM mass, then falls along scri⁺.** `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 01:18:43]` — [L7](../lectures/07-Yx7PWgfC1dw-digest.md)
- **`N_z` is to angular momentum what `m_B` is to energy and momentum** — the sentence that makes the superrotation construction feel inevitable. `[60U7_P2XJlQ @ 00:04:13]` — [L8](../lectures/08-60U7_P2XJlQ-digest.md)
- **Angular momentum has no canonical definition in general relativity, and that is exactly where BMS ran aground** — you can only talk about the whole infinite-dimensional group, you cannot point to a preferred subgroup. `[F42XbX45Dzw @ 01:04:19]` — [extra-05](../lectures/extra-05-F42XbX45Dzw-digest.md)
- **Four constraints versus infinitely many:** the energy-momentum correlations everyone already accepts are four bits and cannot help with a PhD thesis of information; the new family is infinite. `[F42XbX45Dzw @ 00:49:12]` — [extra-05](../lectures/extra-05-F42XbX45Dzw-digest.md)
- **The soft-hair argument turns on *exact* conservation, not average conservation** — everyone already assumes angular momentum is conserved quantum by quantum in Hawking evaporation. `[wg-4Rm-kk70 @ 00:20:31]` — [extra-02](../lectures/extra-02-wg-4Rm-kk70-digest.md)
- **A black hole cannot be indifferent to these charges, by Gauss's law:** whatever surface you use, some charge sits outside the horizon and charge density crosses it. `[F42XbX45Dzw @ 00:47:03]` — [extra-05](../lectures/extra-05-F42XbX45Dzw-digest.md)
- **A free massive particle asymptotes to a fixed point on the hyperboloid at timelike infinity labelled by `|p|/m` and its direction**, and the hard charge acts by the value of the gauge parameter there. `[xZ2FG1kLb9I @ 00:58:49]`, `[xZ2FG1kLb9I @ 01:05:07]` — [L4](../lectures/04-xZ2FG1kLb9I-digest.md)
- **He admits his charge normalisation, chosen so charges come out as integers, is awkward for the field-theory half of the course.** `[yeMZKViadhw @ 01:13:38]` — [L2](../lectures/02-yeMZKViadhw-digest.md)
- **The no-magnetic-monopole assumption is flagged as temporary and load-bearing, with a promise that "everything is going to get corrected" once it is dropped.** `[yeMZKViadhw @ 00:27:37]` — [L2](../lectures/02-yeMZKViadhw-digest.md)
- **Duality here is a change of variables to dodge a nasty calculation, not a claimed symmetry of the theory.** `[WxCY-K-Jb4A @ 00:11:50]` — [L5](../lectures/05-WxCY-K-Jb4A-digest.md)
- **Newman and Penrose had a similar tower of conserved quantities at higher orders in `1/r` in the 1960s, and he says twice in two sentences that he does not understand the relation to his own tower.** `[6kH2pl_zvMA @ 00:49:40]` — [cstq](../lectures/cstq-6kH2pl_zvMA-digest.md)
