## Why they exist

- **There is no preferred sphere at infinity, so general relativity gives you no way to tie together "the same time" at different angles** — everything else follows from that. `[6kH2pl_zvMA @ 00:15:47]` (continues `00:16:49`) — [cstq](../lectures/cstq-6kH2pl_zvMA-digest.md)
- **His own intuition, flagged as such: null infinity is ultralocal.** Its points are out of causal contact, so nothing ties them together, and a principle forcing them to move together would violate causality. `[CdFyoaNv8GI @ 00:37:46]` — [extra-01](../lectures/extra-01-CdFyoaNv8GI-digest.md)
- **BMS set out to *recover* the Poincaré group in a limit and "spectacularly failed"**, finding a much bigger infinite-dimensional group instead. `[F42XbX45Dzw @ 00:05:59]` — [extra-05](../lectures/extra-05-F42XbX45Dzw-digest.md)
- **…and we now know why: general relativity does not reduce to special relativity in the infrared, because it has a spectacularly rich infrared structure that is not there in special relativity.** `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 00:20:34]`, setup at `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 00:18:29]` — [L7](../lectures/07-Yx7PWgfC1dw-digest.md); restated as the vacuum-degeneracy version at `[F42XbX45Dzw @ 01:05:22]` — [extra-05](../lectures/extra-05-F42XbX45Dzw-digest.md)
- **He has no good reason for the transverse piece of the supertranslation vector field**, and declines to invent one — while stressing that the term is indispensable. `[CdFyoaNv8GI @ 00:40:56]` — [extra-01](../lectures/extra-01-CdFyoaNv8GI-digest.md)

## Why they are physical

- **The measuring-device criterion, with a vivid example:** two gravitons leaving the poles, supertranslated so that one departs fifty years early and the other a hundred years late — diffeomorphic, and distinguishable by an instrument. `[6kH2pl_zvMA @ 00:16:49]`, generalised at `[6kH2pl_zvMA @ 00:17:52]` — [cstq](../lectures/cstq-6kH2pl_zvMA-digest.md)
- **The pulse-relabelling example needs no charge computation at all** — "it's clear that they're acting non-trivially on the phase space, just like you boost a black hole you change its energy". He signposts the moment: "it's all about to get really strange". `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 01:01:07]`, `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 00:58:45]` — [L7](../lectures/07-Yx7PWgfC1dw-digest.md)
- **The one-line reply to "it's only a diffeomorphism":** the charges are non-zero, conserved and coordinate invariant, so something coordinate invariant is changing. `[60U7_P2XJlQ @ 00:13:09]` — [L8](../lectures/08-60U7_P2XJlQ-digest.md)
- **Supertranslated Schwarzschild carries classical superrotation charge**, because supertranslations and superrotations do not commute — so the gravitational hair is measurable classically where the electromagnetic version needs interference. `[H4IZ3jwTDp4 @ 01:32:28]` — [L10](../lectures/10-H4IZ3jwTDp4-digest.md)

## Pitfalls

- **You cannot perform a supertranslation "at `u=6`".** A `u`-dependent shift is not a supertranslation — exactly the confusion a careful reader has on first meeting the pulse-relabelling example. `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 01:04:16]` — [L7](../lectures/07-Yx7PWgfC1dw-digest.md)
- **…but energy going out to infinity *does* induce a supertranslation** — the one-sentence version of memory, dropped three lectures early. `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 01:04:16]`, continuing `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 01:05:21]` — [L7](../lectures/07-Yx7PWgfC1dw-digest.md)
- **"Supertranslation shifts `u`" does *not* mean "memory is a clock shift"** — the physical content is the residual deformation on the sphere, and for geodesic observers there is no clock effect at all. `[8Aml1ToQtt4 @ 01:14:41]` — [L9 (guest)](../lectures/09-8Aml1ToQtt4-digest.md)
- **A live "I don't understand this": why `C` transforms with `−2D_z²f` on scri⁺ and `+2D_z²f` on scri⁻.** Nothing is inconsistent; he simply expects a conceptual reason and does not have one. `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 01:15:19]` — [L7](../lectures/07-Yx7PWgfC1dw-digest.md)
- **Supertranslation charges cannot label the degenerate vacua** — the group is abelian, so they all vanish; use `ΔC` and the angular momentum instead. `[CdFyoaNv8GI @ 01:12:01]` — [extra-01](../lectures/extra-01-CdFyoaNv8GI-digest.md)
- **The extra integration function `C|_{scri⁺₋}` is "the point that everybody would have missed"** — specifying the news is not enough, and the missing datum is exactly the vacuum label. `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 01:08:32]` — [L7](../lectures/07-Yx7PWgfC1dw-digest.md)
- **Pieces of the final-data problem are still unsolved, and he says he is getting us only "partway" because of subleading subtleties.** `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 01:06:26]`, `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 01:09:35]` — [L7](../lectures/07-Yx7PWgfC1dw-digest.md)
- **A notation policy stated as a preference:** reuse the same symbols on scri⁻ rather than inventing a parallel alphabet, because a whole new set of symbols "causes more confusion than it clarifies". `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 01:13:01]` — [L7](../lectures/07-Yx7PWgfC1dw-digest.md)
- **He abuses "Bondi mass" for "Bondi mass aspect" out of impatience, and warns you.** `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 00:06:30]` — [L7](../lectures/07-Yx7PWgfC1dw-digest.md)

## History

- **BMS did not think they had a symmetry, and he is candid that he does not understand how they *did* think of it.** `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 01:24:05]` — [L7](../lectures/07-Yx7PWgfC1dw-digest.md)
- **Penrose's asymptotic simplicity roughly forces all the BMS charges to vanish — "a fantastic reduction of the phase space"** — and it was not obvious any solutions satisfied it. `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 01:26:13]`, continuing `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 01:27:20]` — [L7](../lectures/07-Yx7PWgfC1dw-digest.md)
- **Two incompatible failure modes had blocked the field:** asymptotic simplicity was too strong, and the general case seemed to make `C_zz` logarithmically ill-defined at the boundaries — neither route allowed matching past to future BMS frames. `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 01:29:28]` — [L7](../lectures/07-Yx7PWgfC1dw-digest.md)
- **Christodoulou–Klainerman broke the deadlock with an explicit `|u|^{-3/2}` news falloff, which kills the logarithms and makes `C` well defined at the edges — "which was crucial".** `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 01:30:33]`, continuing `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 01:31:38]` — [L7](../lectures/07-Yx7PWgfC1dw-digest.md)
- **The community actively tried to *kill* BMS symmetry with a boundary condition at spatial infinity, and all those attempts had problems.** `[6kH2pl_zvMA @ 00:17:52]` (continues `00:18:53`) — [cstq](../lectures/cstq-6kH2pl_zvMA-digest.md)
- **He does not like Bondi coordinates and uses them anyway, so he can look formulas up** — in gravity the formulas get "exponentially longer" and borrowing them matters more than coordinate elegance. `[OXl7IXT7_Cw @ 01:07:56]`, `[OXl7IXT7_Cw @ 01:09:01]` — [L6](../lectures/06-OXl7IXT7_Cw-digest.md)
- **The BMS boundary conditions "were both too strong and too weak".** `[6kH2pl_zvMA @ 00:34:37]` — [cstq](../lectures/cstq-6kH2pl_zvMA-digest.md)
- **Provenance: the S-matrix symmetry was first announced at Polchinski's 60th birthday conference.** `[CdFyoaNv8GI @ 00:45:21]` — [extra-01](../lectures/extra-01-CdFyoaNv8GI-digest.md)
