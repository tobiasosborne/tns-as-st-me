## What a dressing cloud is, and what it is not

- **The standard mental picture is wrong: the cloud does not travel with the particle.** Photons reach null infinity, massive charges reach timelike infinity, so the cloud disperses; "its real goal in life is to satisfy the conservation law — it's a long distance effect". `[GpN8VAauyj8 @ 00:16:06]` — [extra-04](../lectures/extra-04-GpN8VAauyj8-digest.md)
- **The cloud is highly ambiguous; only its pole structure matters.** It need not be near the particle — it just has to shield the Coulomb field so the conservation law holds. `[GpN8VAauyj8 @ 00:17:10]` — [extra-04](../lectures/extra-04-GpN8VAauyj8-digest.md)
- **Faddeev–Kulish read as an advanced-potential prescription:** they effectively used the advanced potential for all the radiative modes, and the physical prescription is the natural one for incoming and outgoing pieces separately. `[GpN8VAauyj8 @ 00:19:21]` — [extra-04](../lectures/extra-04-GpN8VAauyj8-digest.md)
- **…with the status stated honestly:** the cancellation is checked at leading order only, with no careful all-loops analysis. `[GpN8VAauyj8 @ 00:19:21]` — [extra-04](../lectures/extra-04-GpN8VAauyj8-digest.md)

## Epistemic status of the standard citation

- **Every QFT textbook footnotes Faddeev–Kulish, he doubts the textbook authors read the paper, and he is not persuaded anyone really understands it.** A genuine epistemic complaint about a load-bearing citation. `[OXl7IXT7_Cw @ 00:55:01]` — [L6](../lectures/06-OXl7IXT7_Cw-digest.md)
- **The Faddeev–Kulish basis is probably incomplete and may fail to cluster:** it excludes the states we actually prepare ("at the LHC we don't scatter Faddeev–Kulish states"), unitarity is complicated by superselection sectors, and "it might be that it's unitary but doesn't cluster properly". `[GpN8VAauyj8 @ 00:30:05]` — [extra-04](../lectures/extra-04-GpN8VAauyj8-digest.md)

## Does the soft sector factorise?

- **2015, cautious:** he starts to deny that the Hilbert space at scri factorises, then corrects himself to the weaker and sharper statement — maybe it does factorise, but the S-matrix mixes the factors, so the soft sector cannot simply be disentangled. He flags his own uncertainty. `[CdFyoaNv8GI @ 01:19:22]` — [extra-01](../lectures/extra-01-CdFyoaNv8GI-digest.md)
- **2016, conceded under pressure:** "it's not obvious that you can factorize the Hilbert space … if you're asking me how do I get from here to a complete solution of the information paradox, the answer is I don't know". `[F42XbX45Dzw @ 01:07:26]` — [extra-05](../lectures/extra-05-F42XbX45Dzw-digest.md)
- **2017, sharper still:** entanglement entropy across a cut of scri may not be well defined, since the soft graviton Hilbert space may not admit the factorisation the question presupposes and any candidate cut must transform properly under supertranslations — "I don't know what it means to say late or early". `[GpN8VAauyj8 @ 00:33:36]` — [extra-04](../lectures/extra-04-GpN8VAauyj8-digest.md). *(Collaborator names garbled; content matches Kapec–Raclariu–Strominger, arXiv:1607.07506 — unverified.)*
- **Ignoring the soft sector means you are not describing a unitary process, even for a supernova.** `[CdFyoaNv8GI @ 01:14:08]` — [extra-01](../lectures/extra-01-CdFyoaNv8GI-digest.md)

## The soft mode itself

- **The `ω`-insertion heuristic for the word "soft":** the expression has no `ω` in it, so it is the zero-frequency Fourier component, and being linear in the field it creates or annihilates one zero-energy photon. This is the DC framing. `[yeMZKViadhw @ 00:23:16]` — [L2](../lectures/02-yeMZKViadhw-digest.md)
- **The soft photon mode is paired in the symplectic form with the boundary field that is the *sum* of the endpoint values of the gauge field** — the one step he stops the lecture to make the class compute. `[yeMZKViadhw @ 00:50:44]`, answer at `[yeMZKViadhw @ 00:55:49]` — [L2](../lectures/02-yeMZKViadhw-digest.md)
- **The non-decaying solutions of the massless wave equation are invisible to the standard energy-eigenmode treatment**, and they are exactly what the large gauge parameter needs. `[xZ2FG1kLb9I @ 00:40:52]` — [L4](../lectures/04-xZ2FG1kLb9I-digest.md)
- **An operational protocol for detecting a large gauge transformation:** align two charges' phases, separate, wait, recombine and interfere — his favourite version being a Susskind note using SQUIDs connected by resistors. `[F42XbX45Dzw @ 01:01:02]` — [extra-05](../lectures/extra-05-F42XbX45Dzw-digest.md)
- **Measuring the electromagnetic memory is harder than the gravitational one, because a phase is a quantum observable.** `[8Aml1ToQtt4 @ 01:50:18]` — [L9 (guest)](../lectures/09-8Aml1ToQtt4-digest.md)
