## Why move to the sphere at all

- **Standard quantum field theory is built so that translation invariance is manifest; for this subject the Lorentz symmetry is what matters, and it acts simply on the sphere at infinity.** The flat metric wears translation invariance on its face while Lorentz invariance takes work to check, and the plane-wave basis is a choice made to serve the former. `[xZ2FG1kLb9I @ 00:12:00]` — [L4](../lectures/04-xZ2FG1kLb9I-digest.md)
- **Honest calibration of what the rewriting achieves:** "a lot of what we're doing now is just rephrasing old results in quantum field theory in a different language in which certain of its features, in particular the symmetries, become more evident". `[xZ2FG1kLb9I @ 00:16:18]` — [L4](../lectures/04-xZ2FG1kLb9I-digest.md)
- **He ranks the three formulations:** celestial current algebra is nicer than momentum-space soft theorems and nicer than in/out charges, and more likely to lead somewhere. A bet about which language is productive, not a theorem. `[OXl7IXT7_Cw @ 00:20:17]` — [L6](../lectures/06-OXl7IXT7_Cw-digest.md)
- **The specific late-period reason he prefers celestial currents: "it side steps having to make a gauge choice or a statement about boundary conditions"** — read against his admission that he does not know what the right boundary conditions are, this is the punchline. `[6kH2pl_zvMA @ 00:37:51]` — [cstq](../lectures/cstq-6kH2pl_zvMA-digest.md)
- **The price of the boost-weight basis, stated rather than hidden:** translations no longer act diagonally, they shift boost weight. `[6kH2pl_zvMA @ 00:42:07]` — [cstq](../lectures/cstq-6kH2pl_zvMA-digest.md)
- **A deep-versus-bookkeeping judgment on his own recent work:** "you just use the asymptotic constraint equations of general relativity — that's all it is, but written in a very unfamiliar form … in a form which anybody who studied two-dimensional CFT would say, oh yeah, I know that formula". `[6kH2pl_zvMA @ 00:48:38]` — [cstq](../lectures/cstq-6kH2pl_zvMA-digest.md)

## The status of celestial holography

- **A proven rewriting is not a duality.** That any amplitude can be written as a celestial correlator obeying the symmetries is *proved*, not conjectured; what is open is whether it is useful, and whether the right-hand side can be defined intrinsically. `[6kH2pl_zvMA @ 01:03:27]` — [cstq](../lectures/cstq-6kH2pl_zvMA-digest.md)
- **…with the AdS/CFT comparison that makes the deficiency concrete: "we have a left and a right, they have separate definitions and wherever we can compare them, they agree. Here we only have a left."** `[6kH2pl_zvMA @ 01:04:30]` — [cstq](../lectures/cstq-6kH2pl_zvMA-digest.md)
- **…and a limit on the ambition for the real world:** the string-theory examples work because one knows the theory all the way up, which is not the real world; he hopes instead for toy models where both sides exist. `[6kH2pl_zvMA @ 01:04:30]` — [cstq](../lectures/cstq-6kH2pl_zvMA-digest.md)
- **He marks mid-talk exactly where the established material ends and the speculation begins.** `[6kH2pl_zvMA @ 00:57:11]` — [cstq](../lectures/cstq-6kH2pl_zvMA-digest.md)
- **The symmetry algebra looks over-constrained, and he finds that interesting rather than alarming** — "you wouldn't even think there was a theory that could have all those symmetries … but we know because we can construct examples that they do exist". `[6kH2pl_zvMA @ 01:06:41]` (continues `01:07:43`) — [cstq](../lectures/cstq-6kH2pl_zvMA-digest.md)
- **The programme's origin, inverted:** asked whether celestial holography extends back to AdS, he says the flat-space programme was itself an extension of what had been learned in AdS. `[6kH2pl_zvMA @ 01:07:43]` — [cstq](../lectures/cstq-6kH2pl_zvMA-digest.md)
- **`w_{1+∞}` was not the target:** they derived something messier and only afterwards found the field redefinition that made it recognisable. `[6kH2pl_zvMA @ 00:55:03]` — [cstq](../lectures/cstq-6kH2pl_zvMA-digest.md)
- **Each current in the tower has an observable memory effect, computable algorithmically.** `[6kH2pl_zvMA @ 00:51:49]` — [cstq](../lectures/cstq-6kH2pl_zvMA-digest.md). **TENSION** with the lecture-9 judgment that higher soft moments are *not* memories `[8Aml1ToQtt4 @ 00:18:02]` — [L9 (guest)](../lectures/09-8Aml1ToQtt4-digest.md).

## The level, the central charge, and the stress tensor

- **He does not expect the boundary CFT to be unitary, and says we do not even know whether a level exists or what sign it would have.** Offered without argument, and absent from the notes. `[OXl7IXT7_Cw @ 00:27:28]` — [L6](../lectures/06-OXl7IXT7_Cw-digest.md)
