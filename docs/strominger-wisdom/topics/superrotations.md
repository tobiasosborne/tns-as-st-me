- **His own justification for keeping meromorphic vector fields is pragmatic: "it's good for something"** — the Ward identities give checkable relations among correlators. ## The technical picture

- **The subleading soft factor is finite where the leading one has a pole, because there is a `q` upstairs as well as downstairs**; interpretively, it rotates about the axis through which the particle emerges. `[60U7_P2XJlQ @ 01:16:49]`, `[60U7_P2XJlQ @ 01:17:54]` — [L8](../lectures/08-60U7_P2XJlQ-digest.md)
- **It was guessed, not derived** — from the angular-momentum-flux argument plus uniqueness (it is the only formula vanishing for pure-gauge gravitons), because going straight to momentum space produces terms you cannot tell should cancel. `[60U7_P2XJlQ @ 01:24:25]`, setup at `[60U7_P2XJlQ @ 01:23:20]` — [L8](../lectures/08-60U7_P2XJlQ-digest.md)
- **A geometric picture for why the orthonormal-frame condition excludes boosts:** the boost vector field grows towards the ends of scri even though it fixes the light cone of the origin. `[60U7_P2XJlQ @ 00:23:47]`, `[60U7_P2XJlQ @ 00:24:50]` — [L8](../lectures/08-60U7_P2XJlQ-digest.md)
- **The `4/3` and the `C²` shift in the angular momentum aspect "look kind of funny" and exist only to simplify later formulas** — a signal about which parts of a definition carry physics. `[60U7_P2XJlQ @ 00:03:09]` — [L8](../lectures/08-60U7_P2XJlQ-digest.md)
- **The linear-in-`u` divergence of subleading data is "ubiquitous", traced back to problem set 1.** Third appearance of the phenomenon in the course; naming it as a pattern is the spoken contribution. `[60U7_P2XJlQ @ 00:59:29]` — [L8](../lectures/08-60U7_P2XJlQ-digest.md); first appearance at `[WxCY-K-Jb4A @ 01:42:17]` — [L5](../lectures/05-WxCY-K-Jb4A-digest.md)
- **The route he did not take:** matching classically is harder because you are subtracting a finite piece off a `u`-divergence with ambiguities to control. `[60U7_P2XJlQ @ 01:07:02]` — [L8](../lectures/08-60U7_P2XJlQ-digest.md)
- **Supertranslated Schwarzschild carries classical superrotation charge, because the two do not commute.** `[H4IZ3jwTDp4 @ 01:32:28]` — [L10](../lectures/10-H4IZ3jwTDp4-digest.md)
- **Superrotation charge is what rescues the hard–soft purity argument from the collinear-graviton hole, because it sees the graviton spin.** `[GpN8VAauyj8 @ 00:26:53]` — [extra-04](../lectures/extra-04-GpN8VAauyj8-digest.md)
