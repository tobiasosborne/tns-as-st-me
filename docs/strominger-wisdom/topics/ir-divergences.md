## Divergences as a penalty, not a pathology

- **The organising idea: if a process violates one of the infinitely many conservation laws, quantum field theory sets its amplitude to zero by introducing divergences that exponentiate to `e^{-∞}`.** "There are no real infrared divergences in QED; they're just a penalty that we pay if we are so foolish as to try to compute something that must be zero due to an elementary conservation law." `[GpN8VAauyj8 @ 00:10:50]`, `[GpN8VAauyj8 @ 00:11:55]` — [extra-04](../lectures/extra-04-GpN8VAauyj8-digest.md)
- **Divergences read the other way round: they are the bookkeeping telling you the memory was ignored** — expand around Fock states that pretend no memory occurred and the loops punish you. No systematic cure is known. `[8Aml1ToQtt4 @ 01:00:04]` — [L9 (guest)](../lectures/09-8Aml1ToQtt4-digest.md)
- **He opens the talk by telling the audience the payoff is a reinterpretation**, not a new result — a new-looking viewpoint that ends by recovering Faddeev–Kulish. `[GpN8VAauyj8 @ 00:01:08]` — [extra-04](../lectures/extra-04-GpN8VAauyj8-digest.md)

## The missing S-matrix

- **There is no definition of the S-matrix in QED or the Standard Model**, stated flatly, with Weinberg chapter 13 assigned as the reading. `[lvyyxkjwSCg @ 00:16:13]` — [L1](../lectures/01-lvyyxkjwSCg-digest.md)
- **…but "the S-matrix doesn't exist in non-abelian gauge theory" is, in his view, the *wrong* statement.** The right one is that nobody has constructed it: no one showed it cannot be defined, the usual tricks just failed. `[OXl7IXT7_Cw @ 00:53:59]` — [L6](../lectures/06-OXl7IXT7_Cw-digest.md). Read the two together — the second is the more careful version of the first.
- **Inclusive cross sections are fine for the LHC but have no symmetries — "even energy conservation is not a symmetry of those things".** The sharpest one-line justification for insisting on an S-matrix. `[OXl7IXT7_Cw @ 00:56:04]`, continuing `[OXl7IXT7_Cw @ 00:57:07]` — [L6](../lectures/06-OXl7IXT7_Cw-digest.md)
- **Detector limitations are not an argument against wanting an S-matrix**, and he refuses to accept that unitarity stops above the soft sector: "it's very problematic to assume that unitarity does not extend all the way down to the soft sector". `[WxCY-K-Jb4A @ 01:27:18]`, `[WxCY-K-Jb4A @ 01:28:23]` — [L5](../lectures/05-WxCY-K-Jb4A-digest.md)
- **The S-matrix remains, for him, the only observable in asymptotically flat space** — and a coupled CFT does not help, because coupling to gravity destroys conformality. `[GpN8VAauyj8 @ 00:31:29]` — [extra-04](../lectures/extra-04-GpN8VAauyj8-digest.md)
- **The stated hope for the whole programme:** fifty years of infrared work proceeded without knowing the symmetries that govern precisely that sector, and perhaps they can now shed light on it. Offered as a hope, not a claim. `[WxCY-K-Jb4A @ 01:27:18]` — [L5](../lectures/05-WxCY-K-Jb4A-digest.md)
- **For non-abelian theory the bet is stated with careful modality** — "the hope would be", "the conjecture would be" — that the symmetries allow an S-matrix, or some generalisation, to be defined; the present relations are tree level only. `[OXl7IXT7_Cw @ 00:59:12]`, continuing `[OXl7IXT7_Cw @ 01:00:16]` — [L6](../lectures/06-OXl7IXT7_Cw-digest.md)

## Why non-abelian is worse

- **The physical diagnosis: the problems are long-distance ones, and in QED and gravity the interactions switch off at long distance while in Yang–Mills they persist at arbitrarily long distances.** `[OXl7IXT7_Cw @ 00:58:11]` — [L6](../lectures/06-OXl7IXT7_Cw-digest.md)
- **The non-abelian infrared-finiteness conjecture is offered as a pure extrapolation** — the structure is much more complicated, and the conjecture is that satisfying the conservation laws is nevertheless the whole story. `[GpN8VAauyj8 @ 00:20:26]` — [extra-04](../lectures/extra-04-GpN8VAauyj8-digest.md)
- **Superselection sectors kill Ward identities; the absence of them here is what makes the programme possible.** `[WxCY-K-Jb4A @ 00:42:47]`, `[WxCY-K-Jb4A @ 00:43:50]` — [L5](../lectures/05-WxCY-K-Jb4A-digest.md)

## Cutoffs and what a real measurement can see

- **Every real measurement has an infrared cutoff, and he offers that as part of why the deep infrared structure was missed** — while first insisting that soft theorems are load-bearing for collider calculations. `[wg-4Rm-kk70 @ 00:25:59]` — [extra-02](../lectures/extra-02-wg-4Rm-kk70-digest.md)
- **A concrete open question: how small must the cutoff be before you can claim no information escaped?** His guess, offered as a guess, is below the inverse of the longest time scale in the problem. `[wg-4Rm-kk70 @ 00:28:06]` — [extra-02](../lectures/extra-02-wg-4Rm-kk70-digest.md)
- **LIGO's band is the same problem in hardware:** suspended mirrors give a ~100 Hz band and memory is a zero-frequency effect, so the instrument cannot see it. `[8Aml1ToQtt4 @ 00:12:45]` — [L9 (guest)](../lectures/09-8Aml1ToQtt4-digest.md)
- **The soft sector matters even with no black hole in sight:** a supernova emits arbitrarily soft gravitons too, and ignoring them means you are not describing a unitary process. `[CdFyoaNv8GI @ 01:14:08]` — [extra-01](../lectures/extra-01-CdFyoaNv8GI-digest.md)
- **Nobody knows how to do loop corrections with fields on null infinity as the basic variables** — the stated reason the whole argument is routed through plane waves. `[swrNwD0ypaE @ 00:37:21]` — [L3](../lectures/03-swrNwD0ypaE-digest.md)
- **One-loop corrections to the superrotation story get three candidate readings and no endorsement:** an anomaly, loop-corrected transformation laws, or simply an unexplored subject. `[60U7_P2XJlQ @ 01:26:32]` — [L8](../lectures/08-60U7_P2XJlQ-digest.md)
- **The infrared is where the programme expects to constrain the ultraviolet** — stated as expectation, not result. `[6kH2pl_zvMA @ 00:09:25]` — [cstq](../lectures/cstq-6kH2pl_zvMA-digest.md)
