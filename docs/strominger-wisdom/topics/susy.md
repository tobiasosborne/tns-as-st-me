- **Counting settles the question before any computation: an infinite family cannot be a subgroup of a four-parameter one.** "Before we even start writing anything down it's clear they're going to be there — there's no way that they can be a subgroup of supersymmetry, they must be something completely new." An epistemic move worth noticing: the answer's shape is fixed by a cheap invariant. `[WxCY-K-Jb4A @ 00:58:15]` — [L5](../lectures/05-WxCY-K-Jb4A-digest.md)
- **A joke with content: in a supersymmetric theory every theorem has a superpartner** — used as the motivation for expecting a soft photino theorem at all. `[WxCY-K-Jb4A @ 00:55:05]` — [L5](../lectures/05-WxCY-K-Jb4A-digest.md)
- **Where the fermionic operator "lives": among the superspace objects that trail alongside the supercharge without being it.** Offered as orientation rather than derivation. `[WxCY-K-Jb4A @ 01:16:24]` — [L5](../lectures/05-WxCY-K-Jb4A-digest.md)
- **Off-shell superfield formalisms are not universal; soft theorems are — so there is no guarantee the symmetry looks simple in any particular superfield language.** A clean criterion for when an ugly formula is a fact about physics and when it is a fact about your conventions. `[WxCY-K-Jb4A @ 01:25:12]` — [L5](../lectures/05-WxCY-K-Jb4A-digest.md)
- **The photino charge has a wrinkle he almost walked into:** the components of `Λ` diverge linearly in `u` at the edges of scri, and a delicate projection is needed. "It seems like it would be so easy that I could do it in the next five minutes … but there's a wrinkle … that's an instructive exercise but it takes half an hour not three minutes." `[WxCY-K-Jb4A @ 01:41:13]`, `[WxCY-K-Jb4A @ 01:42:17]`, `[WxCY-K-Jb4A @ 01:43:19]` — [L5](../lectures/05-WxCY-K-Jb4A-digest.md)
- **He ran out of time exactly at that wrinkle**, which is where the derivation of the fermionic charge stops in the recording. — [L5](../lectures/05-WxCY-K-Jb4A-digest.md)
