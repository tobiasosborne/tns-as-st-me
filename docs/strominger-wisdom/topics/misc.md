## Cross-source tensions, collected

Each is kept rather than resolved. Where one is developed for the campaign, the
link points into [campaign-hooks.md](../campaign-hooks.md).

1. **Calibration versus phase space.** Lecture 9 (guest): memory is a change
   against a calibration agreed in advance, and without that agreement the
   question is empty `[8Aml1ToQtt4 @ 01:50:18]`. Strominger: "you don't care
   about experimental device … the flat connections on the sphere at infinity
   are physical" `[F42XbX45Dzw @ 01:02:06]`. Two genuinely different places to
   put the physical content. → [hook H1](../campaign-hooks.md#h1)
2. **Do higher soft moments count as memories?** Lecture 9 (guest): no — only
   the leading moment is a boundary observable, and the rest carry an
   origin-of-time ambiguity `[8Aml1ToQtt4 @ 00:18:02]`, `[8Aml1ToQtt4 @ 00:34:22]`.
   Strominger, late period: yes — "each current has an observable memory effect",
   computable algorithmically at every order `[6kH2pl_zvMA @ 00:51:49]`.
   → [hook H10](../campaign-hooks.md#h10)
3. **The Goldstone / spontaneous-symmetry-breaking dictionary.** The written
   notes use it freely and build a subsection on it; lecture 5 uses it while
   correcting its imprecision `[WxCY-K-Jb4A @ 00:28:53]`; extra-05 hedges it as
   semantics `[F42XbX45Dzw @ 01:10:31]`; lecture 9 (guest) declines to endorse it
   and names the missing counting theorem `[8Aml1ToQtt4 @ 01:43:55]`,
   `[8Aml1ToQtt4 @ 01:44:56]`. Three confidence levels, two speakers, one
   written source.
4. **How firm is antipodal matching?** 2015: "as far as I know there's only one
   Lorentz-invariant CPT-invariant way" `[CdFyoaNv8GI @ 00:57:05]`. 2016 course:
   no first-principles proof, never seen it discussed, uniqueness "probably not
   hard to prove though I haven't tried" `[lvyyxkjwSCg @ 01:26:02]`,
   `[lvyyxkjwSCg @ 01:28:42]`. Lecture 10: it is an assumption, and without it
   there is no theory `[H4IZ3jwTDp4 @ 00:28:37]`. → [hook H4](../campaign-hooks.md#h4)
5. **Does the S-matrix exist?** Lecture 1: "there is no definition of the
   S-matrix in the Standard Model or in QED" `[lvyyxkjwSCg @ 00:16:13]`.
   Lecture 6: that phrasing is wrong — nobody constructed it, and nobody tried to
   prove impossibility `[OXl7IXT7_Cw @ 00:53:59]`. The second is the careful
   version of the first.
6. **AdS: obstruction or ancestor?** extra-01: "anti-de Sitter seems not to have
   a version of this because it's gapped", and studying gravity in a box loses
   the whole structure `[CdFyoaNv8GI @ 00:04:31]`, `[CdFyoaNv8GI @ 01:07:45]`;
   lecture 10 gives the sharp technical disanalogy `[H4IZ3jwTDp4 @ 01:39:19]`.
   Against that, the Green's function for massive QED *is* the AdS₃
   bulk-to-boundary propagator, and CSTQ says the flat-space programme was an
   extension of what had been learned in AdS `[6kH2pl_zvMA @ 01:07:43]`. Not a
   contradiction, but the two readings pull opposite ways.
   → [hook H3](../campaign-hooks.md#h3)
7. **Early versus mature period.** extra-03 (mid-2013) records positions later
   abandoned: no antipodal matching, scri⁻ set aside, "we don't have a Hilbert
   space at scri plus", a deliberate refusal to construct global charges, a level
   `k = 2/e²` that does not survive, and "boundary photons" as a name that did
   not. Read it as evidence about how the mature view was reached, not as the
   mature view.
