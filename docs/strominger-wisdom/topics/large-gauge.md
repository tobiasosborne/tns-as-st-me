## The basic move

- **The standard slogan "physical states are invariant under all gauge transformations" is only true for transformations that are trivial at infinity** — large gauge symmetries act non-trivially on the Hilbert space precisely because they do not die off. `[yeMZKViadhw @ 01:14:42]` — [L2](../lectures/02-yeMZKViadhw-digest.md)
- **Extending the gauge parameter into the bulk: any extension gives a true relation, but only a good one gives a useful relation** — the freedom is real, and the work is in spending it well. `[yeMZKViadhw @ 00:15:40]` — [L2](../lectures/02-yeMZKViadhw-digest.md)
- **A good gauge choice is not one that is correct — correctness is guaranteed — but one where the answer comes out without large cancellations you cannot explain.** `[xZ2FG1kLb9I @ 00:32:22]` — [L4](../lectures/04-xZ2FG1kLb9I-digest.md)
- **The charge identity holds for *any* `ε` going to one at infinity; you pick the one that reveals the physics you want** — you could even choose `ε` to vanish at some charges and not others. `[H4IZ3jwTDp4 @ 00:25:26]` (continues `00:26:29`) — [L10](../lectures/10-H4IZ3jwTDp4-digest.md)
- **Why not a `u`-dependent gauge parameter:** you can, but after a long analysis the charge turns out to depend only on the value at the past boundary of scri⁺, and the difference of two such parameters generates an identically vanishing charge. `[swrNwD0ypaE @ 00:28:52]`, `[swrNwD0ypaE @ 00:30:53]` — [L3](../lectures/03-swrNwD0ypaE-digest.md)
- **The massless wave equation has solutions that do not fall off at null infinity at all, and the standard energy-eigenmode basis hides them** — exactly the solutions the large gauge parameter needs, and a surprise to him. `[xZ2FG1kLb9I @ 00:40:52]` — [L4](../lectures/04-xZ2FG1kLb9I-digest.md)

## The flat connection at infinity

- **The `U=1` assumption is one textbooks make silently:** comparing a red quark at the north pole with a red antiquark at the south pole only makes sense given a flat connection on the sphere at infinity to transport with. `[OXl7IXT7_Cw @ 00:16:02]` — [L6](../lectures/06-OXl7IXT7_Cw-digest.md)
- **…and it is already uncomfortable, because soft gluons are precisely what move you between inequivalent flat connections.** He flags the tension immediately rather than deferring it — the seed of colour memory. `[OXl7IXT7_Cw @ 00:17:06]`, continuing `[OXl7IXT7_Cw @ 00:18:10]` — [L6](../lectures/06-OXl7IXT7_Cw-digest.md)
- **Strominger's strongest statement of where the physical content sits:** "the flat connections on the sphere at infinity are physical, you have to specify a flat connection, and that is a physical thing which has meaning" — said while brushing aside a question about the apparatus. `[F42XbX45Dzw @ 01:02:06]` — [extra-05](../lectures/extra-05-F42XbX45Dzw-digest.md). **TENSION** with the lecture-9 view that memory exists only relative to a calibration `[8Aml1ToQtt4 @ 01:50:18]` — [L9 (guest)](../lectures/09-8Aml1ToQtt4-digest.md); see [campaign-hooks.md](../campaign-hooks.md#h1).
- **Knowing `F` everywhere in Minkowski space is not a solution of the classical scattering problem** — that problem is a map from `A_z` data on scri⁻ to `A_z` data on scri⁺, and the field strength alone underdetermines it. `[OXl7IXT7_Cw @ 00:47:41]`, continuing `[OXl7IXT7_Cw @ 00:48:41]` — [L6](../lectures/06-OXl7IXT7_Cw-digest.md)
- **How anyone got away with fifty years of non-abelian gauge theory without ever stating the matching condition: it is built into the Feynman propagator.** `[OXl7IXT7_Cw @ 00:52:56]` — [L6](../lectures/06-OXl7IXT7_Cw-digest.md)

## Gauge choice, gauge invariance, and what survives

- **The surface integral is gauge invariant; the volume integral requires a gauge.** "If you're not going to choose a gauge you're only going to be able to discuss the surface integral … the minute you want to discuss a volume integral you must choose some gauge." `[H4IZ3jwTDp4 @ 01:04:03]` — [L10](../lectures/10-H4IZ3jwTDp4-digest.md)
- **…and the fallback if you refuse to choose one:** the result does not depend on the picture, only the picture does — you stop at "it's obvious that there must be some charges going into the black hole". `[H4IZ3jwTDp4 @ 01:01:59]` (continues `01:03:01`) — [L10](../lectures/10-H4IZ3jwTDp4-digest.md)
- **What is gauge invariant is the *statement*, not the derivation of it:** "there's a soft photon on the horizon" is gauge invariant; understanding it as a shift in `A_z` induced by the shock wave required choosing a gauge. `[H4IZ3jwTDp4 @ 00:58:43]` — [L10](../lectures/10-H4IZ3jwTDp4-digest.md)
- **Deliberate isolation strategy: throw away the zero mode to expose the new structure.** A net-neutral, angle-dependent shell is chosen precisely because the `ℓ=0` piece only reproduces something already known. `[H4IZ3jwTDp4 @ 00:53:23]` — [L10](../lectures/10-H4IZ3jwTDp4-digest.md)

## Phases, and why the electromagnetic case took a century

- **In electromagnetism the action is on phases, which are "slippery quantum mechanical objects"** — his explanation for why QED went a hundred years without anyone noticing, and an argument for teaching gravity first. `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 01:02:09]`, continuing `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 01:03:14]` — [L7](../lectures/07-Yx7PWgfC1dw-digest.md); the same explanation given independently at `[F42XbX45Dzw @ 01:01:02]` — [extra-05](../lectures/extra-05-F42XbX45Dzw-digest.md)
- **Only phase *differences* were ever measurable in gauge theory; the gravitational analogue has forty years of literature behind it under the name memory.** `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 01:05:21]`, `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 01:06:26]` — [L7](../lectures/07-Yx7PWgfC1dw-digest.md)
- **The non-abelian case makes the necessity of the connection visible classically, where the abelian case hides it behind quantum phases.** `[OXl7IXT7_Cw @ 00:49:47]`, continuing `[OXl7IXT7_Cw @ 00:50:50]` — [L6](../lectures/06-OXl7IXT7_Cw-digest.md)
- **Measuring the electromagnetic version is a quantum measurement**, which is why it is harder than the gravitational one. `[8Aml1ToQtt4 @ 01:50:18]` — [L9 (guest)](../lectures/09-8Aml1ToQtt4-digest.md)
- **The magnetic conservation law is entirely classical, and he finds it surprising that it went unnoticed for a century and a half.** `[WxCY-K-Jb4A @ 00:20:18]` — [L5](../lectures/05-WxCY-K-Jb4A-digest.md)
