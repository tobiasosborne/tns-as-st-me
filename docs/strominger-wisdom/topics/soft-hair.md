## The argument

- **The one-step version:** once you say "black holes are characterised by angular momentum" and "BMS is a symmetry", infinitely many charges follow immediately, because there is no preferred angular momentum — the BMS generators differ by conjugation with supertranslations and are all on the same footing. `[H4IZ3jwTDp4 @ 00:12:43]` — [L10](../lectures/10-H4IZ3jwTDp4-digest.md)
- **"Hair" means a distinguishing feature, nothing more** — a label that separates otherwise identical states. Worth keeping, because the word invites a picture of structure sitting near the horizon. `[wg-4Rm-kk70 @ 00:17:15]` — [extra-02](../lectures/extra-02-wg-4Rm-kk70-digest.md)
- **The argument turns on *exact*, not average, conservation** — angular momentum is already assumed exactly conserved quantum by quantum in Hawking evaporation, and the claim is that infinitely many more charges sit in the same position. `[wg-4Rm-kk70 @ 00:20:31]` — [extra-02](../lectures/extra-02-wg-4Rm-kk70-digest.md)
- **A Gauss's-law observation, not a computation:** whatever surface you use, some charge sits outside the horizon and charge density crosses it, so there is no consistent picture in which the black hole ignores these charges. `[F42XbX45Dzw @ 00:47:03]` — [extra-05](../lectures/extra-05-F42XbX45Dzw-digest.md)
- **The consequence is deterministic, not probabilistic:** charge conservation gives an exact relation between the evaporation products of `|M⟩` and `Q_ε|M⟩`, not a relation on average. `[H4IZ3jwTDp4 @ 01:12:50]` — [L10](../lectures/10-H4IZ3jwTDp4-digest.md)
- **Four constraints versus infinitely many** — the energy-momentum correlations everyone accepts are four bits and cannot absorb a thesis of information. `[F42XbX45Dzw @ 00:49:12]` — [extra-05](../lectures/extra-05-F42XbX45Dzw-digest.md)
- **A horizon soft photon costs no energy and commutes with the Hamiltonian, but shifts angular momentum by one unit, so the state must be orthogonal.** `[H4IZ3jwTDp4 @ 00:39:12]` — [L10](../lectures/10-H4IZ3jwTDp4-digest.md)
- **Gravity is harder than electromagnetism for a simple physical reason: there are no zero-energy waves.** You can send in a shell with zero net charge but non-zero charge moments; you cannot send in a shell with zero net energy. `[H4IZ3jwTDp4 @ 01:16:12]` — [L10](../lectures/10-H4IZ3jwTDp4-digest.md)
- **…but gravity wins on measurability of the hair:** electromagnetic soft hair shows up as phases needing an interference experiment, whereas supertranslation hair changes the *classical* superrotation charge, because supertranslations and superrotations do not commute. `[H4IZ3jwTDp4 @ 01:32:28]` — [L10](../lectures/10-H4IZ3jwTDp4-digest.md)
- **The memory cross-link, made out loud:** the Green's function inverting `D²(D²+2)` for the supertranslating shock wave is the same one relating stress-energy flux to the memory effect. `[H4IZ3jwTDp4 @ 01:22:40]` — [L10](../lectures/10-H4IZ3jwTDp4-digest.md)
- **The degenerate vacuum as a storage medium — "like a big hard drive"**, immediately downgraded from "perfectly possible" to "not ruled out". `[F42XbX45Dzw @ 00:44:58]` — [extra-05](../lectures/extra-05-F42XbX45Dzw-digest.md)

## Scope and honesty

- **"We have not solved the information paradox. I can't say this often enough."** `[wg-4Rm-kk70 @ 00:23:42]` — [extra-02](../lectures/extra-02-wg-4Rm-kk70-digest.md)
- **The picture only exists because the example was chosen to be simple:** for colliding or orbiting black holes "there isn't going to be some nice gauge that will enable you to get some simple physical picture". `[H4IZ3jwTDp4 @ 00:58:43]` (continues `00:59:49`) — [L10](../lectures/10-H4IZ3jwTDp4-digest.md)
- **The interior story is a chosen picture, not a result:** the surface integral is gauge invariant, the volume integral requires a gauge, and the gauge-invariant content is only that charge flows into the hole. `[H4IZ3jwTDp4 @ 01:04:03]`, `[H4IZ3jwTDp4 @ 01:01:59]` — [L10](../lectures/10-H4IZ3jwTDp4-digest.md)
- **What is gauge invariant is the statement, not the derivation of it.** `[H4IZ3jwTDp4 @ 00:58:43]` — [L10](../lectures/10-H4IZ3jwTDp4-digest.md)
- **The zero mode is deliberately discarded to expose the new phenomenon** — throwing net charge into a black hole only tells you it becomes charged, which was already known. `[H4IZ3jwTDp4 @ 00:53:23]` — [L10](../lectures/10-H4IZ3jwTDp4-digest.md)
- **Soft hair is unlikely to store all the information — "there are many more things we need to take into account … but we're very far from that".** `[F42XbX45Dzw @ 00:58:42]` — [extra-05](../lectures/extra-05-F42XbX45Dzw-digest.md)
- **Purity is restored between hard and soft quanta rather than between early and late** — with the collinear-graviton hole in the argument named without prompting. `[GpN8VAauyj8 @ 00:24:41]`, `[GpN8VAauyj8 @ 00:26:53]` — [extra-04](../lectures/extra-04-GpN8VAauyj8-digest.md)
