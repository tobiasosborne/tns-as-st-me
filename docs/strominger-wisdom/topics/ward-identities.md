## What a Ward identity is here

- **Two working definitions, both given in answer to questions:** the charges commute with the S-matrix — which, since `S = lim exp(iHT)`, is the same as commuting with the Hamiltonian; and, more loosely, "any relationship between objects that is implied by symmetries". The second is the definition that transfers to settings without an S-matrix. `[yeMZKViadhw @ 01:16:52]` and `[yeMZKViadhw @ 01:20:08]` — [L2](../lectures/02-yeMZKViadhw-digest.md)
- **The plainest statement of what the conservation law asserts, with no indices and no soft factors:** if in-state `X` evolves to out-state `Y`, then the large-gauge transform of `X` evolves to the large-gauge transform of `Y`. `[yeMZKViadhw @ 01:17:55]` — [L2](../lectures/02-yeMZKViadhw-digest.md)
- **A symmetry has to act on in-states *and* out-states — that is what makes the identity useful, and it is why the matching condition is needed.** `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 01:25:10]` — [L7](../lectures/07-Yx7PWgfC1dw-digest.md)
- **Supertranslation charge conservation is energy conservation at every angle** — not merely total energy in equals total energy out, but the flux from the south pole integrated over all advanced time equalling the flux at the north pole. `[6kH2pl_zvMA @ 00:22:05]` — [cstq](../lectures/cstq-6kH2pl_zvMA-digest.md); the same reading as "the geometry really remembers a lot" at `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 01:43:27]` — [L7](../lectures/07-Yx7PWgfC1dw-digest.md)
- **"Energy conservation at every angle drives people crazy" — and he knows it**, having already fought the same argument a year earlier. `[CdFyoaNv8GI @ 00:48:35]`, `[CdFyoaNv8GI @ 00:49:42]` — [extra-01](../lectures/extra-01-CdFyoaNv8GI-digest.md)
- **Soft gluon theorem as a Kac–Moody Ward identity on the celestial sphere is, in his ranking, the nicest of the three formulations** — nicer than momentum space, nicer than in/out charges, and more likely to lead somewhere. `[OXl7IXT7_Cw @ 00:20:17]` — [L6](../lectures/06-OXl7IXT7_Cw-digest.md)
- **The contribution of the recent celestial work is repackaging, not new physics:** "you just use the asymptotic constraint equations of general relativity … but written in a very unfamiliar form" that a 2d-CFT person recognises. `[6kH2pl_zvMA @ 00:48:38]` — [cstq](../lectures/cstq-6kH2pl_zvMA-digest.md)

## What has and has not been established

- **The bracket computation is explicitly labelled semi-classical, and the quantum question is outsourced:** the identity holds provided the charges are conserved quantum mechanically and free of anomalies, with verification handed to Feynman diagrams rather than attempted in the scri language. `[yeMZKViadhw @ 01:19:06]` and `[yeMZKViadhw @ 01:22:17]` — [L2](../lectures/02-yeMZKViadhw-digest.md)
- **What was proved is a symmetry of the S-matrix, not of the Lagrangian** — the Lagrangian statement would need a long argument about surface terms on which people disagree. `[WxCY-K-Jb4A @ 00:30:02]` — [L5](../lectures/05-WxCY-K-Jb4A-digest.md)
- **Superselection sectors are the hinge: with them you get no useful Ward identities, without them you do** — and here there are none, because it costs only a finite amount of energy to go from one vacuum to another. `[WxCY-K-Jb4A @ 00:42:47]`, `[WxCY-K-Jb4A @ 00:43:50]` — [L5](../lectures/05-WxCY-K-Jb4A-digest.md)
- **Nobody knows how to do loop corrections in a formalism whose basic variables are fields on null infinity** — his stated reason for abandoning the scri language and translating everything into plane waves, where a century of QED can be reused. `[swrNwD0ypaE @ 00:37:21]` — [L3](../lectures/03-swrNwD0ypaE-digest.md)
- **Deriving Weinberg's theorem from the supertranslation Ward identity is "five pages of algebra" that he declines to repeat in class.** `[Yx7PWgfC1dw @ 01:39:04]` — [L7](../lectures/07-Yx7PWgfC1dw-digest.md)
- **The Kac–Moody level check is set as homework, not asserted:** compute the two-current correlator, take the double soft limit, look for a double pole — and there isn't one. `[OXl7IXT7_Cw @ 00:25:18]` — [L6](../lectures/06-OXl7IXT7_Cw-digest.md)
- **Which side of the contour is "inside" does not matter, because the full correlator has to be a singlet** — and reversing the choice picks up residues, i.e. extra soft insertions, giving a second equally true relation. `[OXl7IXT7_Cw @ 00:30:51]`, `[OXl7IXT7_Cw @ 00:38:13]` — [L6](../lectures/06-OXl7IXT7_Cw-digest.md)
- **The soft-hair consequence is emphasised as *deterministic*, not statistical:** charge conservation relates the evaporation products of `|M⟩` and `Q_ε|M⟩` exactly, not on average. `[H4IZ3jwTDp4 @ 01:12:50]` — [L10](../lectures/10-H4IZ3jwTDp4-digest.md)
